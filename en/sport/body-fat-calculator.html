What body fat percentage is

Body fat percentage is the share of fat tissue in your total body mass. Unlike BMI, it tells fat from muscle, so it shows fitness better: two people of the same weight and height can have very different body composition. The rest of the mass is lean (fat-free) mass: muscle, bone, organs and water.

The US Navy method: how it works

The method was developed for the US Navy as a quick estimate that needs no special equipment. Men enter height, neck and waist circumference, women also add hip circumference. The metric formula (cm): men: 495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077·lg(waist − neck) + 0.15456·lg(height)) − 450; women use the sum of waist and hips minus neck with different coefficients. For a man of 178 cm with a 38 cm neck and an 85 cm waist the result is 16.4%, and for a woman of 165 cm with a neck of 32, a waist of 74 and hips of 98 cm it is 28.4%.

How to measure correctly

Measure the neck below the Adam's apple, with the tape slightly angled down at the front. For men the waist is measured at the navel, for women at the narrowest point (usually a little above the navel), after a normal exhale without sucking in. For women the hips are measured at the widest part of the buttocks. The tape should be snug but not press into the skin. To compare over time, measure at the same time of day with the same tape: even one centimetre at the waist visibly changes the result.

Categories for men and women

The calculator uses the American Council on Exercise (ACE) categories. For men: essential fat 2–5%, athletes 6–13%, fitness 14–17%, average 18–24%, obese from 25%. For women: 10–13%, 14–20%, 21–24%, 25–31% and from 32%. The limits differ because women need more essential fat for health, so the scale on the page is built separately for each sex. These are guides, not a diagnosis: they do not depend on age and do not replace a doctor's advice.

An estimate from BMI and age

Without a tape you can use the formula of Deurenberg and colleagues (1991): body fat, % = 1.20·BMI + 0.23·age − 10.8·sex − 5.4, where sex is 1 for men and 0 for women. For a 30-year-old man of 80 kg and 178 cm (BMI 25.2) it gives 21.0%. The formula is for adults, its standard error in the authors' study is about 4 percentage points, and in people with obesity it slightly overestimates fat. It cannot tell muscle from fat, so for trained people it is less accurate than circumferences.

Fat and lean mass, a goal in percent

The third tab turns the percentage into kilograms. For 80 kg and 20% fat that is 16.0 kg of fat and 64.0 kg of lean mass; for a height of 178 cm the fat mass index (FMI) is 5.0 kg/m² and the fat-free mass index (FFMI) is 20.2 kg/m². If you set a goal of 15%, the calculator computes the weight at the goal with lean mass unchanged: 64.0 / (1 − 0.15) = 75.3 kg, so about 4.7 kg of fat to lose. This is a simplification: in a calorie deficit part of the weight is also lost from muscle, so combine the calculation with strength training and the calorie calculator.

Accuracy and limits

All three ways give an estimate, not a measurement: the circumference method usually errs by a few percentage points compared with DEXA or hydrostatic weighing, and the BMI formula is even less accurate. The result is affected by measurement, hydration, where fat is stored and anatomy. Very low values (below essential fat) are dangerous for health. For decisions about health, diet or treatment see a doctor.

This is a formula-based estimate, not a medical test: body composition is measured more accurately by DEXA, bioimpedance or hydrostatic weighing. Discuss health questions with a doctor.

FAQ

How do I calculate body fat percentage?

Measure your neck, waist (and hips for women) and height and enter them on the “US Navy method” tab. For a man of 178 cm with a 38 cm neck and an 85 cm waist the result is 16.4%.

What body fat percentage is normal?

By ACE categories: 14–24% for men (fitness and average), 21–31% for women. Athletes have lower values, and from 25% in men and 32% in women it is obesity.

Why are the limits higher for women?

Women need more essential fat for hormonal and reproductive function, so their scale is shifted up by roughly 8–10 percentage points.

How accurate is the US Navy method?

It is an estimate: it usually errs by a few percentage points compared with laboratory methods. It is better to compare your own results over time.

Where do I measure the waist?

For men at the navel, for women at the narrowest point (usually a little above the navel), after an exhale without sucking in. Use the same place every time.

How is it different from BMI?

BMI depends only on weight and height and cannot tell muscle from fat, while body fat percentage shows body composition. That is why trained people can have an “overweight” BMI with normal fat.

What is the Deurenberg formula?

An estimate of fat from BMI, age and sex: 1.20·BMI + 0.23·age − 10.8·sex − 5.4. The standard error is ≈ 4 percentage points and the formula is for adults only.

What is lean mass?

Everything except fat: muscle, bone, organs and water. For 80 kg at 20% fat the lean mass is 64 kg.

What are FMI and FFMI?

The fat mass index and fat-free mass index: the mass of fat or lean mass in kg divided by the square of height in metres. They depend on height less than percentages do.

How do I calculate weight at a target body fat percentage?

With lean mass unchanged: weight = lean mass / (1 − goal). For 80 kg and 20% at a goal of 15% it is 75.3 kg, i.e. 4.7 kg of fat less.

Is a very low body fat percentage safe?

No, below the essential level (about 2–5% for men, 10–13% for women) it is dangerous for health. The calculator warns if a goal or result is below it.

Does the norm depend on age?

ACE categories do not account for age, although the fat share usually grows over the years. For an individual norm consult a doctor or coach.
