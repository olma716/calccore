How to find the century of a year

A century is 100 years, and the first century covers years 1 to 100, the second 101 to 200, and so on. So the number of the century is the year divided by 100 and rounded up: ⌈year / 100⌉. The year 1991 belongs to ⌈19.91⌉ = 20, the 20th century; the year 2000 is still the 20th century and the 21st began in 2001; the year 988 is the 10th century. The same holds for millennia: ⌈year / 1000⌉.

Centuries BC

For years BC centuries are counted backwards: the 1st century BC is the years 100…1 BC, the 2nd is 200…101 BC. The year 44 BC (the death of Julius Caesar) is in the 1st century BC. Importantly, there is no year zero: 1 BC is followed directly by AD 1, so the span from 1 BC to AD 1 is one year, not two. From 44 BC to AD 2026 2069 years have passed. The “Span between years” tab applies this rule automatically.

Roman numerals

Roman numbers use seven symbols: I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50, C = 100, D = 500, M = 1000. A smaller symbol before a larger one is subtracted (IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40), after it — added (VI = 6, XII = 12). One symbol is not repeated more than three times in a row: 4 is IV, not IIII. So 1994 is MCMXCIV (M + CM + XC + IV), 2024 is MMXXIV, and the largest number in the standard notation is 3999 (MMMCMXCIX). The calculator detects the input automatically: digits are converted to Roman numerals and Roman symbols to Arabic ones.

Eras: anno mundi and ab urbe condita

Besides “from the birth of Christ”, other counts were used in history. The Byzantine “from the creation of the world” era starts the year on 1 September and equals AD + 5508 from January to August and + 5509 from September to December; it was used in Rus until the reform of Peter I. So 2026 is 7534 (January–August) or 7535 (September–December). The AUC era (ab urbe condita) counts from the founding of Rome in 753 BC, and AD 2026 is AUC 2779. The Julian period, introduced by Scaliger for astronomy, gives 6739 for 2026.

Centuries and Roman numerals in text

Centuries are usually written with Roman numerals, often abbreviated: “XXI cent.”, and millennia likewise. The calculator shows both forms: Arabic (21) and Roman (XXI), and also tells which third of the century the year is in (beginning, middle or end) and which decade it is.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is to think the 21st century began in 2000. It began on 1 January 2001, because the first century started with year 1, not year 0. The second is to confuse “the 1920s” (1920–1929) with “the 20th century” (1901–2000). The third is to enter BC dates as negative numbers: the calendar has no year zero, so the calculator takes a BC year as a normal number with a separate era choice.

There is no year zero: 1 BC is followed directly by AD 1. A century starts with year 1 (2000 is still the 20th century). The Byzantine year changes on 1 September.

FAQ

How do I find the century of a year?

Divide the year by 100 and round up: 1991 → 20 (20th century), 2001 → 21 (21st century).

Which century is the year 2000 in?

The 20th: a century ends with a year divisible by 100. The 21st century began on 1 January 2001.

How do I find the years that belong to a century?

From (N − 1)·100 + 1 to N·100. For the 21st century that is 2001–2100.

How do I find a century BC?

In the same way by the BC year number, but the years run backwards: the 1st century BC is 100…1 BC.

Is there a year zero?

No: in the AD/BC system 1 BC is followed directly by AD 1. Astronomers label 1 BC as year 0.

How many years are between 44 BC and AD 2026?

2069 years: 43 up to the notional zero plus 2026 from it (44 − 1 + 2026).

How do I write 1994 in Roman numerals?

MCMXCIV: M (1000) + CM (900) + XC (90) + IV (4).

Why is 4 written IV and not IIII?

In the standard notation a symbol repeats at most three times, so subtraction is used instead of IIII: IV.

What is the largest number in Roman numerals?

3999 (MMMCMXCIX) in the standard notation. Larger numbers used a bar over the symbols, which the calculator does not support.

What is the Byzantine era?

A count of years from the “creation of the world” (5508 BC) in which the year started on 1 September. It was used in Kyivan Rus and Byzantium.

What does AUC mean?

Ab urbe condita — “from the founding of the city”: a count from the founding of Rome, traditionally 753 BC. AD 2026 is AUC 2779.

Can I enter a Roman numeral with similar Cyrillic letters?

Yes, the Cyrillic look-alikes of X, I, C and M are recognised as the Latin letters.
