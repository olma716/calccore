What the Number in Words calculator does

The calculator writes a number or a money amount in words. You can choose hryvnias, US dollars, euros or a plain number, the language — English or Ukrainian — and the format of cents: digits (“56 cents”) or words (“fifty-six cents”). For several amounts at once there is the “List of amounts” tab: each line is processed separately. Numbers of up to 18 integer digits are supported — up to hundreds of quadrillions.

Singular and plural forms

In English the currency name is plural except for exactly one: “one dollar”, “two dollars”, “one cent”, “fifty cents”. Tens and units are joined with a hyphen (“thirty-four”) and “and” stands before the cents. In Ukrainian the noun form depends on the last digits and the numeral gender changes with the noun (one hryvnia is feminine, one dollar masculine, one euro neuter); the calculator handles this automatically in both languages.

Examples

$1,234.56 → “One thousand two hundred thirty-four dollars and 56 cents”. $1.01 with cents in words → “One dollar and one cent”. $21 → “Twenty-one dollars and 00 cents”. $1,000,000 → “One million dollars and 00 cents”. The first letter is capital by default; switch it off when the amount goes into the middle of a sentence.

A number without a currency

With “No currency” an integer is written in words (“twelve thousand three hundred forty-five”) and a decimal is read with “point”: 2.5 is “two point five” and 0.05 is “zero point zero five”. The Ukrainian reading names the fraction instead: whole parts plus tenths, hundredths or thousandths.

When an amount in words is needed

In contracts, invoices, powers of attorney and payment documents an amount is often written both in digits and in words to prevent mistakes and forgery. The exact requirements — for example whether the cents are written in digits — depend on the document type and the organisation, so check them. The calculator helps to get the text quickly and avoid grammar mistakes.

Common mistakes

The most frequent mistakes are forgetting the hyphen in compound numbers (“twenty one” instead of “twenty-one”), using “and” inside hundreds in a way that differs between British and American style, and writing the plural of one: “one dollars”. The calculator follows one consistent style: no “and” inside the number, “and” only before the cents, and a hyphen between tens and units. If your document needs the British “one hundred and five”, add the word by hand.

Ukrainian currency in English

The Ukrainian hryvnia is “hryvnia” (plural “hryvnias”) and its hundredth is “kopiyka” (plural “kopiykas”); the calculator uses these words. For dollars and euros the hundredths are “cents”. If the Ukrainian version of the words is chosen, the same amount is written in Ukrainian grammar, with the euro staying unchanged.

The fractional part of an amount is rounded to hundredths; without a currency up to 6 decimal places are supported. Check the requirements of your document for writing amounts.

FAQ

How do I write an amount in words?

Enter the amount on the “Amount in words” tab and choose the currency and language. For 1,234.56 USD the result is “One thousand two hundred thirty-four dollars and 56 cents”.

Is it “one dollar” or “one dollars”?

Exactly one is singular: “one dollar”, “one cent”. Everything else is plural: “two dollars”, “fifty cents”.

How do I write cents?

By default in digits (“56 cents”). Choose “in words” to get “fifty-six cents”.

Does the calculator write hyphens in numbers?

Yes, English numbers from 21 to 99 use a hyphen: “twenty-one”, “thirty-four”.

How do I write a decimal number?

Choose “No currency”: 2.5 becomes “two point five” and 0.05 “zero point zero five”.

What is the largest supported number?

Up to 18 integer digits, that is up to 999,999,999,999,999,999 (hundreds of quadrillions).

How do I write a negative amount?

Put a minus sign before the number: the result starts with the word “minus”.

Can I enter spaces between digit groups?

Yes, spaces between groups of digits are ignored: “1 234 567.89” is read correctly.

What if there are more than two decimals?

For a currency the amount is rounded to hundredths: 1.999 gives “Two dollars and 00 cents”. Without a currency up to 6 decimal places are supported.

Can I process several amounts at once?

Yes, use the “List of amounts” tab: enter one amount per line and each is written in words; the whole result can be copied.

Can I get the Ukrainian text on the English page?

Yes. The language of the words is chosen separately from the page language, so you can write an amount in Ukrainian or English on either version.

Is the result suitable for official documents?

The text follows the rules of the language, but the requirements for writing amounts in a particular document (capital letters, format of cents) are set by the organisation or law, so check the result against them.
