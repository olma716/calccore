What the NMT score converter does

The calculator converts NMT test (raw) points to rating points on the 100–200 scale used for admission. The “One subject” tab shows the result and the full conversion table, “All four subjects” computes the three compulsory subjects and one elective together and checks the thresholds, and “Points needed” solves the reverse task: how many tasks you must get right to reach a score.

What the NMT is and which subjects it has

The National Multi-subject Test (NMT) replaced the ZNO for admission. It consists of Ukrainian language, mathematics and history of Ukraine, plus a fourth subject of your choice: a foreign language (English, German, French or Spanish), biology, physics, chemistry, geography or Ukrainian literature. In 2026 the fourth subject can be chosen regardless of the specialty, but every programme has a profile subject with a higher weight.

The 100–200 scale and the threshold

Each subject has its own table: the number of tasks done correctly (test points) corresponds to a score from 100 to 200. The minimum that gives 100 is the threshold: 8 test points in Ukrainian, 5 in mathematics, 9 in history of Ukraine, 5 in a foreign language, 7 in biology, 5 in physics and chemistry, 7 in geography and 7 in Ukrainian literature. The maximum — 45, 32, 54, 32, 46, 32, 32, 46 and 45 test points respectively — gives 200. If the threshold is not passed, the result in that subject is not used for admission.

Conversion examples

Mathematics: 5 test points is 100, 12 is 140, 20 is 152, 25 is 170, 32 is 200. Ukrainian language: 8 is 100, 20 is 144, 36 is 170, 45 is 200. History of Ukraine: 9 is 100, 30 is 150, 41 is 170, 54 is 200. Foreign language: 5 is 100, 18 is 150, 25 is 162, 32 is 200. The relation is non-linear: the first steps above the threshold give many points, the middle of the scale is “stretched” and at the top each test point is expensive again.

Why tables differ by subject and year

The tests differ in difficulty, so test points are equated with a separate table for each subject, so that 100–200 mean a comparable level of knowledge. The tables are approved together with the Admission Procedure for each year, and the values for NMT 2026, 2025 or 2024 do not coincide. This calculator uses the tables of NMT 2026; for admission with the results of other years take the tables of that year.

What to do with the result

Scores on the 100–200 scale go into the competitive score formula together with the weights of your specialty. Use the “Admission score” calculator for that: it computes the score, compares different fourth subjects and shows which score you need for a goal.

The tables are for NMT 2026 from open data (checked against two sources); the final values are in the annex to the Admission Procedure. Tables for other years differ.

FAQ

What is the NMT?

The National Multi-subject Test: a single admission test of three compulsory subjects (Ukrainian language, mathematics, history of Ukraine) and one elective.

How does the NMT differ from the ZNO?

The ZNO was taken separately for each subject, while the NMT is a single multi-subject test. The 100–200 scale remained, but the tables for converting test points are different.

What are test points and rating points?

Test points are the points earned for the tasks done. Rating points are the result on the 100–200 scale into which the test points are converted by the table of the subject.

What is the NMT threshold?

A minimum rating score of 100. It corresponds to a different number of test points in different subjects: for example 5 in mathematics or 8 in Ukrainian.

How many test points give 200?

The maximum: 45 in Ukrainian, 32 in mathematics, 54 in history of Ukraine, 32 in a foreign language, 46 in biology and geography, 32 in physics and chemistry, 45 in Ukrainian literature.

Why do the tables differ between subjects?

Because the tests differ in difficulty and number of tasks, and the table brings the results to a comparable scale.

Are the tables the same every year?

No. They are approved for each admission campaign, so for NMT 2025 or 2024 the values are different. The calculator uses the tables of NMT 2026.

Which subjects can be the fourth?

A foreign language (English, German, French or Spanish), biology, physics, chemistry, geography or Ukrainian literature.

What if the threshold is not passed?

The result of that subject is not used for admission. The details depend on the admission rules, so check them.

How do I calculate the competitive score?

Put the scores on the 100–200 scale and the weights of the specialty into the score formula: the “Admission score” calculator does it automatically.

How many test points are needed for 170?

It depends on the subject: for example 25 test points in mathematics, 36 in Ukrainian, 41 in history of Ukraine, 36 in biology.

How accurate are the tables?

The tables were checked against two open sources for NMT 2026; the official values are in the annex to the Admission Procedure and should be checked before applying.
