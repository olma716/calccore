How the ovulation day is calculated

Ovulation happens about 14 days before the next period starts (the luteal phase). For a period starting on March 1, 2026 and a 28-day cycle the next period is March 29, 2026 and ovulation is March 15, 2026 (Sunday). With a 12-day luteal phase ovulation moves to March 17, 2026.

Cycle length changes the ovulation day: with a 35-day cycle it falls on March 22, 2026, with a 24-day cycle on March 11, 2026. So the calculator counts from the end of the cycle, not from its “14th day”.

The fertile window and the peak chance

Conception is possible only in the six-day window ending on the ovulation day: sperm survive in the female body for up to 5 days and the egg for 12–24 hours. For ovulation on March 15, 2026 that is March 10 – March 15.

The chance is highest on the two days before ovulation and on the ovulation day (March 13 – March 15); at the start of the window it is much lower.

An irregular cycle and tests

If your cycle varies from 26 to 32 days, ovulation falls between March 13 and March 19 and the fertile window between March 8 and March 19. Fill in the “shortest” and “longest” cycle to get such a range.

Ovulation tests are usually started a few days before the expected ovulation, and a pregnancy test is taken from the day of the expected period. For exact timing use LH tests, basal body temperature and cervical mucus observation.

The calculator gives an estimate, is not a contraceptive method and does not replace a doctor’s consultation. The fertile window follows Wilcox et al. (1995); a 14-day luteal phase is an average and yours may differ.

FAQ

How do I calculate the ovulation day?

Subtract the luteal phase (usually 14 days) from the date of the expected next period. For a period starting on March 1, 2026 and a 28-day cycle ovulation is on March 15, 2026.

What is the fertile window and how long does it last?

It is the six days ending on the ovulation day: conception is possible only in this period. For ovulation on March 15, 2026 the window is March 10 – March 15.

When is the chance of getting pregnant the highest?

On the two days before ovulation and on the ovulation day — for ovulation on March 15, 2026 that is March 13 – March 15. At the start of the window the chance is much lower.

How does ovulation change with a shorter or longer cycle?

It moves with the cycle length: with 35 days it is March 22, 2026, with 24 days March 11, 2026 (for a period starting on March 1, 2026).

How long do the egg and sperm live?

The egg can be fertilized for 12–24 hours after ovulation, and sperm stay active in the female body for up to 5 days. That is why the window starts 5 days before ovulation.

How do I calculate ovulation with an irregular cycle?

Enter the shortest and the longest cycle: for 26–32 days ovulation falls on March 13 – March 19 and the window on March 8 – March 19. For accuracy it is better to use ovulation tests.

What is the luteal phase and is it always 14 days?

It is the interval between ovulation and the start of the next period. The average is 14 days; in different women it is 12–16 days. For 12 days and a 28-day cycle ovulation is on March 17, 2026. Change the value in the additional parameters.

When should I take an ovulation test and a pregnancy test?

Ovulation tests are started a few days before the expected ovulation (the calculator suggests the date 5 days before it). A pregnancy test is more reliable from the day of the expected period.

Can the calculator be used as contraception?

No. The calculation is approximate, and the ovulation day can shift because of stress, illness and other factors. To prevent pregnancy consult a doctor.

How accurate is the calculator?

The accuracy is about ±1–2 days with a regular cycle. The forecast for later cycles is less accurate than for the nearest one. For exact timing use ovulation tests and a doctor’s advice.
