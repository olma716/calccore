What the converter does

The converter converts values between 130 units in 12 categories: length, mass and weight, volume, area, speed, temperature, time, pressure, energy, power, angle and digital data. Enter a number, choose the source and target units, and you see the result, the conversion factor, the inverse factor and all the other units of the category. The value can be entered with a comma or a point, with a sign and in a form like 1e-3.

Exact factors

The factors follow the exact definitions. An inch is 2.54 cm, a pound 0.45359237 kg, an ounce 28.349523125 g, a mile 1.609344 km, a nautical mile 1852 m, an acre 4046.8564224 m² (0.40468564224 ha), an atmosphere 101.325 kPa, a kilowatt-hour 3.6 MJ, a calorie 4.184 J and a knot 1.852 km/h. So converting there and back gives the same number, without tails like 0.453592.

US and British units

Gallons, pints and fluid ounces differ between the US and Britain. A US gallon is 3.785411784 L while the British (imperial) one is 4.54609 L, and a US pint is 473.176473 ml against 568.26125 ml. So in the converter they are split into the groups “US” and “British”. The same goes for tons: the short (US) ton is 2000 pounds and the long (British) ton 2240 pounds.

The cup and the spoon

A cup has no single size: the metric one is 250 ml, then 200 ml, the US legal 240 ml, the US customary 236.6 ml and the British 284.1 ml. All five are in the volume list, as are the teaspoon (5 ml) and the tablespoon (15 ml) and the US spoons. To convert cups and spoons to grams by ingredient there is the cooking measurement converter.

Temperature

Temperature is converted through kelvin because the scales have different zeros: K = °C + 273.15, K = (°F + 459.67) × 5/9, K = °R × 5/9. So 0 °C is 32 °F, 273.15 K and 491.67 °R, 100 °C is 212 °F and −40 °C equals −40 °F. A temperature below absolute zero is rejected. For an oven there is the oven temperature converter.

Digital data and time

In data, decimal prefixes (a kilobyte is 1000 B, a gigabyte 10⁹ B) are distinguished from binary ones (a kibibyte is 1024 B, a gibibyte 2³⁰ B): 1 GiB is 1.073741824 GB. Bits and bytes are related as 1 B = 8 bits. In time a year is the Julian one (365.25 days, 31,557,600 s) and a month is the average (a year / 12), so it is marked approximate.

Significant digits

In the settings you can choose 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 or 15 significant digits. The calculation runs at full precision and only the display is rounded; the sign “≈” means the value is rounded. Very large and very small numbers are shown as a mantissa and a power of ten.

Limits

The parsec and the average month are approximate, and conversion between units of different nature (for example mass and weight as a force) is not done. For legal and trade calculations use the official tables and the full values without rounding.

The factors are exact by the SI and NIST definitions, and a cup and a gallon come in several kinds: US, British, metric. The parsec and the average month are approximate.

FAQ

How many centimeters are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 cm by definition, so 1 inch is 0.0254 m.

How many kilograms are in a pound?

Exactly 0.45359237 kg. An ounce is 28.349523125 g, and a pound has 16 ounces.

How many liters are in a gallon?

A US gallon is 3.785411784 L and a British (imperial) one 4.54609 L, so these gallons differ.

How many kilometers are in a mile?

Exactly 1.609344 km, so 1 mile is 5280 feet. A nautical mile is 1852 m.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32: 0 °C is 32 °F, 100 °C is 212 °F and −40 °C equals −40 °F. The converter works through kelvin.

How many square meters are in an acre?

4046.8564224 m², which is 0.40468564224 ha.

What is the difference between kB and KiB?

A kilobyte (kB) is 1000 bytes and a kibibyte (KiB) 1024 bytes. So 1 GiB equals 1.073741824 GB.

How many milliliters are in a cup?

It depends on the choice: the metric cup is 250 ml, the US one 240 ml (customary 236.6 ml), the British 284.1 ml, and there is also 200 ml.

How many seconds are in a year?

A Julian year (365.25 days) has 31,557,600 s. The month in the converter is the average (a year divided by 12) and approximate.

How many kilocalories are in a kilojoule?

1 kcal is 4.184 kJ by the definition of the thermochemical calorie, so 1 kJ ≈ 0.239 kcal.

Which horsepower is more exact?

There is a metric one (735.49875 W) and a mechanical one (745.69987 W), and the converter has both.

Why does the result sometimes show ≈?

It means the value is rounded to the chosen number of significant digits. Raise it in the settings to see more digits.
