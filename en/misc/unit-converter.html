Why you need a unit converter

In everyday life, it's common to need conversion between measurement systems — reading a recipe from a foreign site, checking a product specification, or working with international documents. This converter instantly shows the value in all common units of the chosen category.

Metric vs imperial system

The metric system (kilometers, kilograms, liters, Celsius) is the official standard in the vast majority of countries worldwide. The imperial system (miles, pounds, gallons, Fahrenheit) remains the primary system in the US, and some of its elements (like miles) are still used in the UK.

Conversion categories

  • Length: meters, kilometers, centimeters, miles, feet, inches, yards.
  • Weight: kilograms, grams, pounds, ounces, tons.
  • Volume: liters, milliliters, gallons, quarts, pints, cups.
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin.
  • Area: square meters, square kilometers, hectares, acres, square feet.
  • Speed: km/h, mph, m/s, knots.

Conversion values are based on standard international factors and are highly accurate for practical use.

FAQ

What's the difference between metric and imperial systems?

The metric system (kilometers, kilograms, liters) is used in most countries worldwide, while the imperial system (miles, pounds, gallons) is mainly used in the US.

Why does temperature convert differently from other units?

Unlike length or weight, temperature scales have different zero points, so conversion requires an addition/subtraction formula rather than simple multiplication.
