How the weekday is found

The calculator converts the date to a Julian day number (JDN), a continuous count of days, and takes the remainder after dividing by 7. For November 6, 2026 that is a Friday, the 310th day of 365 in the year. For July 20, 1969 the result is a Sunday. Besides the name of the day the calculator shows how many days remain or have passed from today, the week number under ISO 8601, the quarter, whether the year is a leap year and whether the day is a weekend or a weekday.

The Julian calendar for historical dates

Until 1582 Europe used the Julian calendar, and in Ukraine and Russia it stayed in force until 1918. If you enter an old date in the Gregorian calendar you get a nominal (proleptic) result. That is why you can choose the calendar: January 1, 1500 in the Julian calendar was a Wednesday and corresponds to January 10 in the Gregorian one, whereas in the Gregorian calendar it would be a Monday. The calculator also shows the same date in the other calendar, for example December 25, 1917 (Julian) is January 7, 1918 (Gregorian), and both are a Monday.

The switch of 1582

Weekdays did not shift at the switch between calendars: Thursday October 4, 1582 in the Julian calendar was followed by Friday October 15 in the Gregorian one. That is why October 5–14, 1582 simply do not exist in the countries that moved to the new style then, and the calculator warns you about it. For dates before 1582 the Gregorian result is only nominal.

Week number and day of the year

The week number follows ISO 8601: a week starts on Monday and the first week of the year contains January 4. So January 1, 2026 is week 1 of 2026, December 31, 2026 is week 53 and December 30, 2024 already belongs to week 1 of 2025. The day of the year is shown with the year length: the 310th of 365 for November 6, 2026. The ISO week number is shown for Gregorian dates.

The same date over the years

The second tab shows which weekdays your date falls on over a range of up to 100 years: for birthdays, holidays and anniversaries. For example, August 24 from 2026 to 2035 falls on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Below the table it counts how many times each weekday occurred. February 29 exists only in leap years, so for other years the calculator says there is no such date.

Find the Nth weekday of a month

The third tab finds a date by the rule “the Nth weekday of a month”: the third Sunday of May 2027 is May 16, the fourth Thursday of November 2026 is November 26, the last Monday of May 2026 is May 25. That is how movable holidays and event dates are worked out. You can also list all such days of the month at once. If a month has no fifth such day, the calculator tells you.

Fridays the 13th

The “Find a date” tab counts Fridays the 13th over a range of years. In 2026 there are three: in February, March and November. There are never more than three in a year. The calendar repeats every 400 years and over that period the 13th falls on a Friday 688 times, more often than on any other weekday.

Limits

Years from 1 to 9999 are allowed. For dates before the Gregorian calendar was introduced the result depends on when a country switched to the new style. Only Saturday and Sunday are treated as weekends: public holidays and transferred working days are not taken into account.

For dates before 1582 choose the calendar that was used at the time. Saturday and Sunday count as weekends, with no public holidays.

FAQ

How do I find the weekday of a date?

Enter the day, month and year: the calculator shows the weekday. For example, November 6, 2026 is a Friday.

What weekday was my birthday?

Enter your date of birth: the result shows the weekday and how many days have passed. The second tab shows which weekday your birthday falls on in the coming years.

Does the calculator work for future dates?

Yes, for any date up to the year 9999. It shows how many days until the date.

Why choose the Julian calendar?

For historical dates: until 1582 Europe used the Julian calendar, and Ukraine and Russia until 1918. January 1, 1500 was a Wednesday in it, but a Monday in the proleptic Gregorian calendar.

Do weekdays break at the switch between calendars?

No. Thursday October 4, 1582 (Julian) was followed by Friday October 15 (Gregorian).

How is the week number counted?

By ISO 8601: a week starts on Monday and the first week of the year contains January 4. January 1, 2026 is week 1, and December 30, 2024 is already week 1 of 2025.

What does the day of the year mean?

It is the ordinal number of the day in the year: November 6, 2026 is the 310th of 365, and a leap year has 366.

How do I find the third Sunday of May or the fourth Thursday of November?

Open the “Find a date” tab, choose “The Nth weekday of a month” and give the year, month, number and weekday. The third Sunday of May 2027 is May 16.

How many Fridays the 13th are there in a year?

From one to three. In 2026 there are three: February 13, March 13 and November 13. Over 400 years there are 688.

Why is there no result for February 29 in some years?

February 29 exists only in leap years: in a range the calculator skips the other years and shows how many there are.

Are public holidays taken into account?

No. Saturday and Sunday count as weekends, and the calculator does not know public holidays or transferred working days.

What are the earliest and latest dates?

Years from 1 to 9999, in the Gregorian or Julian calendar. For dates before 1582 the Gregorian result is nominal.
