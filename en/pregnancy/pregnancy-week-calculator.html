How the pregnancy week is calculated

The medical term (gestational age) is counted from the first day of the last menstrual period. For an LMP of January 1, 2026 the term on April 1, 2026 is 12 weeks 6 days, that is the 13th week of pregnancy. The week of pregnancy is one more than the completed weeks because the count starts from week zero.

To find the term on another date, for example the day of an ultrasound, open the “Term on another date” block: for an LMP of January 1, 2026 the term on August 15, 2026 will be 32 weeks 2 days. Other methods, the trimesters and the due date are in the due date calculator.

The weeks table, the baby’s size and milestones

The table shows all weeks 1–42 with “from–to” dates: for an LMP of January 1, 2026 the 20th week falls on May 14 – May 20. For every week the baby’s size is given as a comparison with fruit and vegetables and a typical development milestone.

These are reference guides, not a medical measurement: real sizes vary. Look for the due date, the trimesters and other methods of calculation in the due date calculator.

Weeks, months and trimesters

Doctors count pregnancy in weeks and call the months obstetric: 1 month = 4 weeks, so 40 weeks are 10 obstetric months. The months correspond to the weeks like this: 1st — weeks 1–4, 2nd — 5–8, 3rd — 9–12, 4th — 13–16, 5th — 17–20, 6th — 21–24, 7th — 25–28, 8th — 29–32, 9th — 33–36, 10th — 37–40. The calculator shows the term both in months and in weeks with days.

Trimesters by ACOG: the first lasts up to 13 weeks 6 days, the second from 14 weeks 0 days to 27 weeks 6 days, the third from 28 weeks. A pregnancy usually lasts 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of the last menstrual period; it is considered full term from 37 weeks.

The calculator is a reference: the fruit comparisons and the development milestones are guidelines, not a medical measurement. Your doctor confirms the term.

FAQ

How do I find out the pregnancy week?

Enter the first day of the last menstrual period: for an LMP of January 1, 2026 the term on April 1, 2026 is 12 weeks 6 days, that is the 13th week of pregnancy.

Why is “12 weeks 6 days” the 13th week?

The term shows fully completed weeks and days, while the 13th week is the one in progress. So the week of pregnancy is one more than the completed weeks.

What will the term be on a particular date?

Open the “Term on another date” block and choose the day: for an LMP of January 1, 2026 the term on August 15, 2026 will be 32 weeks 2 days. The date must be between the first day of the period and a term of 42 weeks.

How do I find which dates a week falls on?

Look at the weeks table: for an LMP of January 1, 2026 the 20th week is May 14 – May 20. The current week is highlighted in the table.

How is the pregnancy week different from the fetal age?

The pregnancy week is counted from the first day of the last menstrual period, while the fetal age is counted from conception, about 2 weeks less. Doctors and the calculator use the gestational age.

How accurate are the baby’s sizes?

The fruit comparison is a guide for clarity, not a measurement: the real size is individual and is assessed by the doctor on an ultrasound. The term is confirmed by the doctor.

How many months is the 20th week of pregnancy?

The 20th week is 19 completed weeks and 0–6 days, that is 4 obstetric months and 3 weeks of fully completed term; the week belongs to the 5th obstetric month (weeks 17–20). An obstetric month is 4 weeks, not a calendar month.

How many weeks does a pregnancy last?

Typically 40 weeks (280 days) from the first day of the last menstrual period. By the ACOG classification, early term is 37 weeks 0 days – 38 weeks 6 days, full term is 39 weeks 0 days – 40 weeks 6 days, late term is 41 weeks 0 days – 41 weeks 6 days and post-term is 42 weeks 0 days or more.

When do the second and third trimesters begin?

The second trimester begins at 14 weeks 0 days and the third at 28 weeks 0 days; the first lasts up to 13 weeks 6 days. Look for the due date and the key dates by trimester in the due date calculator.

Why does the ultrasound term differ from the LMP term?

An ultrasound estimates the term from the size of the fetus while the LMP uses the date of the period, so a difference of a few days is possible. The doctor revises the term only if the difference exceeds a threshold that depends on the term. You can compare the LMP and an ultrasound in the due date calculator.
