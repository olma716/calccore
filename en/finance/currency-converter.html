How does the currency converter work?

The CalcCore converter turns an amount from one currency into another using the official National Bank of Ukraine (NBU) rate or bank rates: monobank or PrivatBank. It’s useful for budgeting, travel planning, and accounting calculations.

NBU rates are published according to the bank’s schedule. The calculator also shows the rate date so you know which day the conversion is based on.

Which rates are available?

  • NBU — the official rate of the National Bank of Ukraine, 40+ currencies
  • monobank — buy and sell rates for USD and EUR
  • PrivatBank — the cash buy and sell rates for USD and EUR

Rates table and history charts

Below the converter you will find a table of USD, EUR, GBP, PLN, CHF, CZK and CAD rates from every source, and a chart of the official NBU rate against the hryvnia for 7, 30, 90 days or a year. A separate chart shows prices of the main cryptocurrencies — Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, Solana, XRP and Toncoin — in hryvnias or US dollars.

FAQ

Where does the exchange rate come from?

The rate is loaded from the official NBU API (statistical service) which provides a list of currencies and their rates to UAH.

How is the NBU rate different from bank rates?

The NBU rate is official, set once per day for all operations and used for reporting and calculations. The monobank and PrivatBank rates are the buy and sell rates of a specific bank: they differ from each other, change during the day, and the bank sells currency for more than it buys it for. Branches and exchange offices may use a different rate from all of these.

What do “buy” and “sell” mean in the rates table?

Buy is the rate at which a bank buys currency from you (you receive hryvnias); sell is the rate at which it sells currency to you (you pay in hryvnias). The sell rate is usually higher than the buy rate, and the difference is the bank’s margin. The NBU rate has no buy/sell split.

Where does the rate history chart come from?

The currency chart is built from official NBU rates against the hryvnia for a chosen period: 7, 30, 90 days or a year. The cryptocurrency chart shows market prices in hryvnias or US dollars based on CoinGecko data.

Why can the rate date be “tomorrow”?

NBU publishes rates on a schedule: before ~15:30 it may be the current business day rate, after ~15:30 it’s often the next business day rate.

What if NBU is temporarily unavailable?

The calculator may use the latest saved rates from your browser (localStorage), if available.

Where do cryptocurrency rates come from?

NBU does not publish cryptocurrency rates, so they are loaded from CoinGecko — market prices in UAH refreshed every few minutes. Currencies in the “Currencies” list use the official NBU rate.

Can I convert currencies beyond USD/EUR?

Yes. The dropdown includes all currencies returned by the NBU service for the selected rate date, plus the main cryptocurrencies.

Is this suitable for accounting purposes?

It’s great for estimates. For official records, follow your organization’s rules on the rate date and rounding methodology.
