Why you need an add days calculator

This tool helps you quickly find an exact date in the future or past without manually counting on a calendar. It's especially useful for long periods (months, years), where it's easy to make mistakes with manual calculations.

Practical uses

  • Document validity: when a power of attorney, warranty, or visa expires, if you know the validity period from the issue date.
  • Event planning: what date it will be 90 days from now for an important deadline or event.
  • Medical timelines: calculating the date of a next appointment or the end of a treatment course.
  • Financial calculations: loan repayment date or deposit maturity date.

How month and year calculations work

When adding months or years, the calculator keeps the same day of the month whenever possible. For example, adding 1 month to January 31 gives February 28 (or 29 in a leap year), since February doesn't have 31 days. This is standard behavior for calendar calculations.

This calculator uses the standard Gregorian calendar for all calculations.

FAQ

How do I calculate a date N days from now?

Choose a start date, enter the number of days (or weeks or months), and click "add" — the calculator will show the exact future date.

Can I subtract days instead of adding?

Yes, select the "subtract" mode to find out what date it was a certain number of days ago.

Does it account for months of different lengths?

Yes, when adding months, the calculator correctly handles varying month lengths and year-end transitions.
