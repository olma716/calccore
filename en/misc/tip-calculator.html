What the calculator does

The calculator works out the tip, the total and the amount per person. Enter the bill and the percentage (the buttons 5, 10, 15 and 20% or your own) or a fixed tip amount, and the number of people. The result shows the tip, the total, the effective tip percentage and the calculation step by step. All amounts are worked out in cents, so there are no rounding errors.

How the tip is worked out

The tip is the bill times the percentage. For a bill of 100 and 15% it is 15, the total is 115 and for 4 people it is 28.75 per person. If the bill has tax, you can add it as a percentage and choose which amount the tip is worked out from: before tax or after. For 10% tax a 15% tip on the amount before tax gives 15 and a total of 125, and on the amount after tax 16.50 and a total of 126.50.

Rounding the amount per person

The amount per person often comes out with cents or an awkward figure. You can round it to the nearest whole or up to a whole, to 5 or 10. The difference from rounding is added to the tip, and the calculator shows the effective percentage. For example 28.75 per person rounded up to 5 is 30: a total of 120, a tip of 20 and effectively 20%.

Unequal split by orders

When guests ordered dishes of different cost, an equal split is unfair. On the “Unequal split” tab enter a name and an amount on each line (“Olga 30”), add shared dishes that are split equally, and the tip percentage. Tax and tip are shared in proportion to each person's part. For example Olga 30, Ivan 50, Masha 25 and shared 20 give a bill of 125, a 10% tip of 12.50 and a total of 137.50. The cents are distributed so that the sum matches to the cent.

How much to tip

Tipping customs differ by country, type of venue and even city. By common practice in the US a restaurant tip is usually around 15–20% of the bill. In many European countries service is already included in the bill, so people leave less, round the amount up or leave nothing, and in some countries tipping is not customary. Check the bill: sometimes service is already in it. A general overview is on the Tipping page of Wikipedia.

Limits

The calculator does not know local rules: in some countries a tip or a service charge is already added to the bill, and in others tax is worked out separately. For several currencies choose the symbol in the settings: rates are not converted. The maximum is 100 people and 30 lines in the unequal split.

Tipping is voluntary and customs differ by country and venue: check whether service is already in the bill. Rounding the amount per person adds the difference to the tip, and the effective percentage is shown separately.

FAQ

How much should I tip?

It depends on the country and the venue. By common practice in the US it is about 15–20% of the bill, in many European countries less, and sometimes service is already in the bill.

How do I work out a tip from the bill?

Multiply the bill by the percentage: 100 × 15% = 15. The calculator adds the tip to the bill and divides the sum by the number of people.

Is the tip worked out before or after tax?

It depends on custom. The calculator lets you choose the base: for 10% tax a 15% tip on the amount before tax is 15 and on the amount after tax 16.50 for a bill of 100.

How do I split a bill equally?

Add the tip to the bill and divide the sum by the number of people: 115 for 4 people is 28.75 per person.

How do I split a bill when the orders differ?

On the “Unequal split” tab enter each person's amount: tax and tip are shared in proportion and shared dishes equally.

What do I do with shared dishes?

Enter their amount in the “Shared dishes” field: it is split equally among everyone and added to each person's part.

How do I round the amount per person?

Choose rounding in “More”: to the nearest whole, up to a whole, to 5 or to 10. The difference goes to the tip and the effective percentage is shown separately.

Can I leave a tip as a fixed amount?

Yes, choose “A fixed amount” in the “Tip” field. The calculator shows the effective percentage of the bill itself.

How do I find out what percentage I tipped?

On the “What percentage” tab enter the bill and the amount paid: the tip and the percentage are worked out automatically.

Why does the amount per person times the people not match the total?

If the division is not even, the remainder in cents stays in the total. The difference does not exceed the number of cents by the number of people.

Can I choose a currency?

Yes, there are the symbols ₴, $, €, £ and zł or no symbol. Exchange rates are not converted.

Does the calculator take local tipping rules into account?

No, it works from the percentages you enter. Check whether a service charge is already in the bill.
