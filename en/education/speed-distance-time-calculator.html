What the motion calculator does

The calculator solves kinematics problems: speed, distance and time for uniform motion, the average speed over several segments, uniformly accelerated motion, free fall and a body thrown vertically upward. For each problem it is enough to enter the known quantities: the calculator chooses the formulas itself and shows the steps.

Speed, distance and time

In uniform motion v = s / t, hence s = v · t and t = s / v. A car that drove 150 km in 2 hours had a speed of 150 / 2 = 75 km/h, that is 125/6 ≈ 20.83 m/s. To convert: 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h, so 36 km/h = 10 m/s. The calculator also works with miles per hour, knots and feet per second.

Average speed

The average speed is the total distance divided by the total time, not the arithmetic mean of the speeds. If 60 km took 1 hour (60 km/h) and the next 60 km took 2 hours (30 km/h), the average speed is 120 / 3 = 40 km/h, while the arithmetic mean would be 45 km/h. The calculator shows both and explains the difference.

Uniformly accelerated motion

With constant acceleration a: v = v₀ + a · t, s = v₀ · t + a · t² / 2, s = (v₀ + v) · t / 2, v² = v₀² + 2a · s. Of the five quantities s, v₀, v, a, t, three are enough. For v₀ = 0, a = 2 m/s² and t = 5 s the displacement is 25 m and the speed 10 m/s. For braking the acceleration is negative: with v₀ = 10 m/s, a = −2 m/s² and s = 16 m the time to reach that point is 2 s.

Free fall

From rest and without air resistance: h = g · t² / 2, v = g · t, v = √(2g · h). With g = 9.8 m/s² a body falls from 19.6 m in 2 s and arrives at 19.6 m/s. In school g = 10 m/s² is often used, so the value of g can be changed in the settings.

A body thrown upward

The maximum height is H = v₀² / (2g), the time up t↑ = v₀ / g, the whole flight back T = 2v₀ / g. For v₀ = 19.6 m/s (g = 9.8) we get H = 19.6 m, the time up is 2 s and the whole flight 4 s. The speed on return equals the initial speed in magnitude.

Average speed is not the arithmetic mean

A typical mistake is to find the average speed as the arithmetic mean of the speeds. In fact v = total distance / total time. If a car drove the first 60 km at 60 km/h (1 hour) and the next 60 km at 30 km/h (2 hours), the average speed is 120 / 3 = 40 km/h, not 45. The “Average speed” mode takes up to six segments, each given by distance and time, speed and time, or distance and speed, and shows the wrong arithmetic mean for comparison.

Motion is considered along a straight line and air resistance is ignored. All numbers are exact and roots are written exactly (for example 7√2).

FAQ

How do I find speed, distance and time?

v = s / t, s = v · t, t = s / v. For 150 km in 2 h the speed is 75 km/h.

How do I convert km/h to m/s?

Divide by 3.6: 36 km/h = 10 m/s. Conversely multiply by 3.6.

How do I find the average speed?

Divide the total distance by the total time. It is not the arithmetic mean of the speeds unless the times on the segments are equal.

Why is the average 40 km/h and not 45?

Because the second half of the way took longer: more time was spent on the slower segment, so it weighs more.

What is acceleration?

The change of speed per unit time: a = (v − v₀) / t. It is measured in m/s².

How do I find the distance in uniformly accelerated motion?

s = v₀ · t + a · t² / 2 or s = (v₀ + v) · t / 2. For v₀ = 0, a = 2 m/s² and t = 5 s it is 25 m.

What does a negative acceleration mean?

The body is braking: the acceleration points opposite to the motion. The calculator accepts a negative a.

How fast does a body fall from height h?

v = √(2g · h). For h = 19.6 m and g = 9.8 m/s² it is 19.6 m/s.

Which value of g should I use?

More precisely 9.81 m/s², often 9.8, and 10 m/s² in school problems. The value is chosen in the settings.

How do I find the maximum height?

H = v₀² / (2g). For 19.6 m/s it is 19.6 m.

Is air resistance taken into account?

No, all the formulas are for ideal motion without resistance.

Why is the answer sometimes written with a root?

Because, for example, the speed √(2g·h) is not always a whole number: √98 = 7√2. A decimal value is shown beside it.

How do I convert km/h to m/s?

Divide by 3.6: 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Conversely, multiply m/s by 3.6.

Why is the average speed not the arithmetic mean?

Because on the slower segments the car spends more time, so they weigh more. The correct way is the total distance divided by the total time.
