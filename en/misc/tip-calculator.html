How much tip is customary to leave

Tipping norms vary significantly by country and venue type:

  • United States: 15-20% of the bill is standard at restaurants, 20%+ for excellent service.
  • Europe: mostly 5-10% or simply rounding up the bill, since service is often already included in prices.
  • Cafes and bars: usually less than full restaurants — 5-10% or rounding.
  • Food delivery: typically 10-15% or a small fixed amount.

How bill splitting is calculated

The calculator first adds the tip to the bill amount to get the total due, then splits that total equally among the number of people. This is the standard approach when everyone ordered roughly the same amount.

When equal splitting doesn't work well

If people ordered dishes of very different value, it's fairer to split the bill proportionally to what each person consumed rather than equally. In that case, it's better to calculate each person's share separately, then add the tip proportionally to their share of the bill.

Tipping norms are a cultural practice, not a strict rule. Follow local customs and the quality of service received.

FAQ

How much tip is customary to leave?

Norms vary by country: in the US, 15-20% is typical, while in most European countries it's 5-10% or simply rounding up the bill, depending on service quality.

Is the tip calculated before or after splitting among people?

The total tip is calculated from the full bill first, then the total amount (bill + tip) is split equally among all participants.

Can I enter a fixed tip amount instead of a percentage?

This calculator computes tips as a percentage, which is the most common approach. For a fixed amount, simply add it manually to the total.
