What the concrete calculator counts

The calculator works out the concrete volume of one or several structural elements (up to six): a slab, strip foundation, wall, beam, rectangular and round column. There are two modes. Ready-mix shows how much to order including the reserve, what it costs with delivery and a pump, and how many trips a mixer makes. Mix it yourself counts cement in bags, sand, gravel and water for your mix. There are no built-in prices: you enter the prices of your suppliers.

Volume formulas of the elements

The volume is the product of the dimensions in metres, multiplied by the number of identical elements. Slab: length × width × thickness, for example 6 × 4 × 0.2 = 4.8 m³. Strip foundation: strip length × width × height, for example 20 × 0.4 × 0.8 = 6.4 m³. Wall: length × height × thickness (10 × 2.5 × 0.3 = 7.5 m³). Beam: length × width × height (6 × 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.72 m³). Rectangular column: height × side a × side b. Round column: π × (diameter / 2)² × height, for example Ø0.3 m and a height of 3 m give 0.21 m³.

Ready-mix: reserve, cost and trips

The order volume is the sum of the elements plus the reserve. For the example with a slab and a strip (11.2 m³) with a 5% reserve you need to order 11.76 m³. At illustrative prices of ₴2,500 per m³, delivery of ₴1,500 and a pump of ₴3,000 that is ₴33,900, or ₴2,883 per m³ with delivery. If you enter a mixer capacity of 8 m³, the calculator shows 2 trips.

Mixing it yourself: cement, sand, gravel, water

The mix is set by volume for every element separately. First the wet volume is multiplied by the dry volume factor (1.54 by default), then divided between cement, sand and gravel in the proportion. Cement in kilograms is its volume times the bulk density of 1,440 kg/m³, bags are rounded up, and the water in litres is the cement in kg × W/C. For 11.76 m³ of a 1:2:4 mix that is 75 bags of cement of 50 kg, 5.17 m³ of sand, 10.35 m³ of gravel and 1,863 L of water at a W/C of 0.5.

Mix and concrete grades

The calculator does not tie the mix to grades (C20/25, B25 and so on): the mix depends on the cement grade, the moisture of the sand and the gravel fraction, and the tables in different sources differ and often do not say whether the proportions are by weight or by volume. Take the recipe of the grade you need from the plant or a lab and enter it as a “Custom mix”.

The calculator gives an approximate estimate, not a recipe. The mix of a given grade is set by the plant or a lab for your cement and aggregates.

FAQ

How do I calculate the concrete volume of a slab?

Multiply the length by the width and the thickness in metres. For 6 × 4 m and a thickness of 0.2 m it is 4.8 m³.

How do I calculate concrete for a strip foundation?

Multiply the total length of the strip (the perimeter plus the inner walls) by the width and the height. For 20 m of a 0.4 × 0.8 m strip it is 6.4 m³.

How do I calculate concrete for a column?

For a round column: π × (diameter / 2)² × height. For a diameter of 0.3 m and a height of 3 m it is 0.21 m³. For a rectangular one: height × side a × side b.

What reserve of concrete should I order?

You set the reserve: the calculator uses 5% by default. For 11.2 m³ that is an order of 11.76 m³.

How many mixer trips are needed?

Divide the order volume by the mixer capacity and round up. For 11.76 m³ and a 8 m³ mixer it is 2 trips.

How much cement per 1 m³ of concrete?

It depends on the mix. By volume with a factor of 1.54 and a density of 1,440 kg/m³, a 1:2:4 mix needs about 316.8 kg (6.3 bags of 50 kg), 1:3:6 needs 221.8 kg and 1:1.5:3 needs 403.2 kg.

How much sand and gravel per 1 m³ of concrete?

For a 1:2:4 mix and a factor of 1.54, about 0.44 m³ of sand and 0.88 m³ of gravel per 1 m³ of concrete.

How much water per 1 m³ of concrete?

The water equals the cement in kg multiplied by the W/C. For 1:2:4 and a W/C of 0.5 it is about 158 L per 1 m³.

Can I set a different mix for different elements?

Yes: in the “Mix it yourself” mode every element has its own mix. For example a 4.8 m³ slab with a 1:2:4 mix (5.04 m³ with the reserve) and a 6.4 m³ strip with a 1:3:6 mix (6.72 m³) need 1,597 kg and 1,490 kg of cement, 3,087 kg in total, that is 62 bags of 50 kg; sand 5.32 m³, gravel 10.64 m³.

What is the dry volume factor?

It is a multiplier for the voids between the grains: more dry material is needed than the finished concrete. Usually 1.52–1.57, 1.54 in the calculator.

Does the calculator have concrete grades such as C20/25?

No: the mix depends on the cement grade and the aggregates, and the tables in the sources disagree. Enter the plant’s recipe as a “Custom mix” or pick a proportion by volume.

Ready-mix or mixing it myself?

At the illustrative prices of the example, ready-mix costs ₴33,900 and mixing costs ₴34,326. Enter your own prices: the difference depends on the region, delivery and your labour.

Does the calculator include rebar?

No, only concrete. Count the rebar in the rebar calculator and the foundation budget in the construction cost calculator.
