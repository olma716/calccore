What is a zodiac sign

The Western zodiac consists of 12 signs, each corresponding to a specific date range during the year, based on the Sun's position relative to constellations at the time of birth. Each sign is associated with an element (fire, earth, air, water) and a ruling planet.

The Chinese zodiac

Unlike the Western zodiac, the Chinese zodiac is determined by birth year using a 12-year animal cycle: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. Each animal symbolizes certain personality traits and has its own element under the Wu Xing system (metal, wood, water, fire, earth).

FAQ

How is a zodiac sign determined?

The Western zodiac sign is determined by day and month of birth — each sign corresponds to a specific date range during the year.

How is the Chinese zodiac different from Western zodiac?

The Chinese zodiac is determined by birth year (a 12-year animal cycle), while the Western zodiac is based on day and month of birth.

Is there scientific evidence for astrology?

No, astrology has no scientific evidence supporting an influence on personality or fate. It's more of a cultural and entertainment tradition.
