How a loan is calculated

An annuity payment is the same every month: P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. For a 200,000 UAH loan at 36% per year for 24 months the payment is 11,809.48 UAH and the overpayment is 83,428 UAH. At the start most of the payment goes to interest, at the end to principal.

In a differentiated schedule the principal is the same every month and the interest falls with the balance. The payment falls from 14,333.33 UAH to 8,583.41 UAH and the overpayment is 75,000 UAH: 8,428 UAH less than with an annuity, but the first payments are larger. All amounts are rounded to the cent and the last payment closes the balance exactly to zero.

Rate per year, per month or per day

Banks usually quote an annual percentage, while installment plans and card loans quote it per month or per day. The calculator converts the rate to a monthly one: an annual rate is divided by 12, a monthly one is used as is, a daily one is multiplied by 365 and divided by 12. For example, 1% per day is nominally 365% per year: a 10,000 UAH loan for a month at that rate costs 3,042 UAH in interest and 13,042 UAH to repay.

The rate in the calculator is nominal, without day-by-day compounding: its exact value and the way interest is charged (by calendar days, on the balance, on the initial amount) are set by the agreement.

Total cost of credit and the real annual rate

Interest overpayment is only part of the cost. The total cost of credit is interest plus all fees and insurance. In the example with a 2% one-time fee and 1% yearly insurance it is 91,428 UAH (45.7% of the amount), while interest alone is 83,428 UAH.

The approximate real annual rate (APR) is estimated as the internal rate of return of the cash flow (the loan received minus the one-time fee, then payments plus fees and insurance) multiplied by 12. In this example it is about 39.84% with a nominal rate of 36%. It is an estimate: the exact formula and value are stated by the lender in the agreement.

The calculation is an estimate. Real loan terms, fees, insurance and the real rate are set by the lender, so check them in the loan agreement. The real annual rate here is approximate.

FAQ

How is the monthly annuity payment calculated?

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where r is the monthly rate. For 200,000 UAH, 36% per year and 24 months it is 11,809.48 UAH and the total overpayment is 83,428 UAH.

How do differentiated payments differ from annuity ones?

A differentiated payment falls: the principal is the same and the interest shrinks. For the same terms it goes from 14,333.33 to 8,583.41 UAH with an overpayment of 75,000 UAH. An annuity gives a constant 11,809.48 UAH payment and a larger overpayment.

How do I convert a daily or monthly rate to an annual one?

The calculator multiplies a monthly rate by 12 and a daily one by 365. For example 1% per day is nominally 365% per year and 3% per month is 36% per year. That lets you compare loans quoted in different units.

What is the total cost of credit and how does it differ from overpayment?

Overpayment by interest is interest only. The total cost of credit adds all fees and insurance. In the example with a 2% one-time fee and 1% yearly insurance the total cost is 91,428 UAH against 83,428 UAH of interest.

What is the real annual rate (APR) and how does the calculator work it out?

It is a rate that counts not only interest but also fees and insurance. The calculator estimates it as the internal rate of return of the payment flow multiplied by 12. In the example it is about 39.84% instead of 36%. It is an estimate, the exact rate is stated by the lender.

How do I include an arrangement fee, a monthly fee and insurance?

Enter a one-time fee (as a percentage of the amount or an amount), a monthly fee (as a percentage of the initial amount or an amount) and insurance (as a percentage of the loan per year or an amount per month). They increase the total cost of credit and the real annual rate.

How do I calculate an installment plan with a fee instead of interest?

Set the rate to 0 and enter a monthly fee as a percentage of the initial amount. For example a 30,000 UAH installment plan for 10 months with a 1.7% monthly fee costs 5,100 UAH (17.0% of the amount) and the real annual rate is about 36%.

How does early repayment reduce the overpayment?

An extra payment reduces the balance, so interest is charged on a smaller amount. In the example an extra 2,000 UAH per month shortens the loan to 20 months and saves 17,082 UAH of interest.

Can I download the schedule to Excel?

Yes. The “Download CSV” button saves the table with unformatted numbers (a dot as the decimal separator, two decimals) that Excel opens as numbers. You can also copy the table and paste it into a sheet.

Why can the result differ from the bank?

It is an estimate from standard formulas. Lenders may count days differently, round, apply fees by their own rules or change the debit date, so check the result against the loan agreement.
