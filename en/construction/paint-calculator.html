How to calculate the amount of paint

The calculation is based on the surface area to be painted (walls, minus doors and windows, and optionally the ceiling), multiplied by the number of coats and paint coverage per square meter. Formula: paint needed = area × number of coats × coverage per m².

How much paint is used per m²

Typical coverage is 0.1-0.15 L/m² per coat, but it depends significantly on:

  • Paint type: water-based, latex, oil-based — different density and coverage properties.
  • Surface: rough or porous surfaces (like plaster) absorb more paint than smooth ones.
  • Color: going from dark to light color or vice versa often requires an extra coat.
  • Application method: roller, brush, or sprayer have different paint usage efficiency.

Exact coverage figures are always listed on the specific paint's packaging — it's best to use those for the most accurate calculation.

Why a second coat of paint is needed

A single coat of paint rarely provides even, saturated color coverage — especially when switching to a contrasting color. A second coat evens out the tone, hides streaks from the roller or brush in the first coat, and improves the finish's durability against wear and washing.

It's recommended to buy about 10% extra paint for surface irregularities, touch-ups, or future minor repairs in the same shade.
