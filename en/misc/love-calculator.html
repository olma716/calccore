What this calculator is

The love calculator is an entertainment game with names. You enter two names and at once see a joke percentage from 1 to 100, its level (from “more like friendship” to “written in the stars”) and a heart that fills by the percentage. The second tab has the old FLAMES game, which says from the same names whether they are friends, lovers, feel affection or are almost married.

How the percentage is worked out

Only the letters of each name are kept, in lowercase, and the names are put in alphabetical order and joined into a pair. The pair is turned into a number by the FNV-1a hash function with an extra mixing of the bits, and the percentage is the remainder of dividing by 100 plus one. So for the same names the result is always the same, and the order of the names does not matter: Sophia and Luke give as much as Luke and Sophia.

The FLAMES game

FLAMES is a well-known joke game. First the shared letters of the two names are crossed out, one from each, then the remaining letters are counted. That number n shows which letter of the word FLAMES to cross out round the circle: Friends, Lovers, Affection, Marriage, Enemies, Siblings. The calculator shows every step, so you can check the game with a pencil.

Names in any language

You can write names in Cyrillic, Latin or other alphabets, in capitals or lowercase, with spaces, hyphens and apostrophes: only letters count. Digits and symbols are ignored. A double name counts as one word of all its letters, and different spellings of one name (for example Olena and Elena) give different results because the letters differ.

Why it is a joke

The percentage has no scientific basis: the calculator knows nothing about you and only shuffles letters. Love and compatibility depend on people, not names. So do not take the result seriously, do not make decisions by it and do not be upset if it is low: with another spelling of the name it would be different.

Limits

The calculator is for entertainment and gives no relationship advice. The names are not sent anywhere: everything is worked out in your browser. The maximum is 40 characters in each name. If the names are made of the same letters, nothing is left in FLAMES and the game has no answer.

It is a joke: a name does not decide how a relationship turns out. The result is a game of letters that is always the same for the same names, so show it to friends, but do not make decisions by it.

FAQ

Can I trust this calculator?

No, it is a joke for fun. The result only reflects a shuffle of the letters in two names and says nothing about real compatibility.

Why is the result always the same for the same names?

The percentage is worked out by a formula with no randomness, so for the same names it is the same every time. Change the spelling of a name and the result changes too.

Does the result depend on the order of the names?

No. The names are put in alphabetical order before the calculation, so Sophia and Luke give the same as Luke and Sophia.

Why do different spellings of one name give a different percentage?

The letters are counted. Olena and Elena are made of different letters, so the result differs.

What is FLAMES?

An old joke game: cross out the shared letters of two names, count what is left and cross out the letters of the word FLAMES. The last letter gives the answer: friends, lovers, affection, marriage, enemies or siblings.

What does the letter in the FLAMES result mean?

F is friends, L is lovers, A is affection, M is marriage, E is enemies, S is siblings. The calculator shows every step of the crossing out.

Why did FLAMES give nothing?

If the names are made of the same letters, all the letters are crossed out and the game has no answer. Try other names.

Which names can I enter?

In any language and alphabet, in capitals or lowercase. Only letters count and digits and symbols are ignored.

Is a surname counted?

You can enter a first name and a surname in one field: all the letters count. But the standard game usually takes first names only.

What do the compatibility levels mean?

From more like friendship to written in the stars: up to 20%, up to 40%, up to 60%, up to 80% and above. They are joke labels, not an assessment of a relationship.

Can I share the result?

Yes, the “Share” button copies a link with both names. Remember the names will be visible to anyone who opens the link.

Are the names I enter stored?

No, everything is worked out in your browser and nothing is sent. The names go into the link only when you press “Share” yourself.
