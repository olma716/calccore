How a mortgage is calculated

The loan amount is the property price minus the down payment. An annuity payment is the same every month: P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual ÷ 12) and n is the number of months. For an apartment worth 2,000,000 UAH with a 20% down payment (400,000 UAH), a 18% rate and 20 years the loan is 1,600,000 UAH, the payment is 24,692.98 UAH and the interest overpayment is 4,326,326 UAH.

In a differentiated schedule the principal is the same every month and the interest falls with the balance: the payment falls from 30,666.67 UAH to 6,765.86 UAH and the overpayment is 2,891,999 UAH. That is 1,434,327 UAH less than with an annuity, but the first payments are noticeably larger. All amounts are rounded to the cent and the last payment closes the balance.

Down payment, loan-to-value and upfront costs

The loan-to-value ratio (LTV) in the example is 80%. The larger the down payment, the smaller the loan, the payment and the overpayment. The “currency / %” switch converts the value: 400,000 UAH out of 2,000,000 UAH is 20%.

Besides the down payment you need the bank fee and one-time purchase costs (notary, valuation, fees) at the start. Their amounts vary, so enter them yourself. With a bank fee of 1% of the loan and one-time costs of 2% of the price you need 456,000 UAH at the start, not just 400,000 UAH of down payment.

Total cost of a mortgage and the real annual rate

Interest overpayment is only part of the cost. The total cost of credit is interest plus the bank fee, insurance and monthly costs. With a 1% fee and 0.5% yearly insurance it is 4,502,327 UAH (281.4% of the loan), while interest alone is 4,326,326 UAH.

The approximate real annual rate (APR) is estimated as the internal rate of return of the cash flow (the loan received minus the fee, then payments plus insurance and costs) multiplied by 12. In the example it is about 18.75% with a nominal rate of 18%. It is an estimate: the exact formula and value are stated by the bank. The total cost of the purchase — down payment, all payments, fees and one-time costs — is 6,542,327 UAH, which is 3.27 times the price of the apartment.

The calculation is an estimate. Real mortgage terms, fees, insurance, the allowed share of income and the real rate are set by the bank, so check them in the loan agreement. The real annual rate here is approximate.

FAQ

How is the monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where P is the loan amount and r is the monthly rate. For an apartment worth 2,000,000 UAH, a 20% down payment, a 18% rate and 20 years it is 24,692.98 UAH and the interest overpayment is 4,326,326 UAH.

Which is better: an annuity or differentiated payments?

An annuity gives a constant payment of 24,692.98 UAH and a larger overpayment (4,326,326 UAH). A differentiated payment falls from 30,666.67 to 6,765.86 UAH with an overpayment of 2,891,999 UAH, but needs a higher income at the start.

What are the down payment and loan-to-value (LTV)?

The down payment is the part of the price you pay yourself. LTV = loan amount ÷ price. For a 20% down payment it is 80%. The minimum down payment and the allowed LTV are set by the bank or the program.

What other costs are there besides the monthly payment?

The bank's arrangement fee, insurance (life and property), a notary, property valuation and state fees. Their amounts depend on the bank and the property, so the calculator does not fill them in: enter your own values and they go into the upfront costs and the total cost.

What are the total cost of credit and APR?

The total cost is interest plus the fee, insurance and monthly costs. In the example with a 1% fee and 0.5% yearly insurance it is 4,502,327 UAH against 4,326,326 UAH of interest, and the approximate real annual rate is about 18.75% instead of 18%. The exact rate is stated by the bank.

How many times more than the price does a mortgage cost?

In the example with fees and costs the total cost of the purchase (down payment, all payments, fees and one-time costs) is 6,542,327 UAH — 3.27 times the price of 2,000,000 UAH. The calculator shows it in the “Total cost of the purchase” block.

How does early repayment reduce the overpayment?

An extra payment reduces the balance, so interest is charged on a smaller amount. If in the example you pay an extra 5,000 UAH every month, the mortgage closes in 9.3 years instead of 20 and the interest savings are 2,632,304 UAH.

What income do I need for such a mortgage?

Banks limit the share of income that goes to payments, usually within 40–50%, but the exact limit is set by the bank. For a payment of 24,693 UAH at a 40% share you need an income of about 61,732 UAH per month. Enter your income in the calculator to see the payment share.

Does the calculator include state programs such as “eOselia”?

No: program terms (rate, down payment, limits) change, so they are not filled in automatically. Enter the rate, down payment and term that apply in your program and you get the calculation for them. Check the current terms with the bank.

Can I download the schedule and how accurate is the calculation?

Yes, the “Download CSV” button saves the table with numbers for Excel. The calculation is an estimate from standard formulas: banks may count days differently, round and apply fees by their own rules, so check the result against the loan agreement.
