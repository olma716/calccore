How the event countdown works

Enter the event date and, if needed, its name and time (00:00 by default): every second the calculator shows how many days, hours, minutes and seconds are left. Below you see weeks and days, total hours, minutes and seconds, the weekday of the event and the number of working days to it. When the event arrives a message appears and the counter switches to counting “since the event”. If the date is already in the past you immediately see how long ago it was.

Time zones

An event is often held in another time zone: a live stream, a release, the start of an online conference. Choose the time zone of the event: Kyiv, London, Berlin or Paris, New York, Los Angeles, Tokyo or UTC. For example, January 1, 2027 at 00:00 in New York arrives at 07:00 in Kyiv. In the solution steps the calculator shows both the event time and the same moment by your clock. Daylight saving time is handled automatically.

Quick dates

Under the fields there are buttons “New Year”, “Christmas (Dec 25)”, “Mar 8”, “Independence Day (Aug 24)” and “Weekend”. They fill in the nearest such date at 00:00, and “Weekend” fills in the nearest Saturday. For moving dates such as Easter, use the separate Easter calculator and enter the date by hand.

The timer

The “Timer” tab works like an ordinary timer: enter hours, minutes and seconds (up to 99 hours 59 minutes 59 seconds) or press a ready value — 1, 5, 10, 15, 25 or 45 minutes. “Start” begins the count, “Pause” keeps the remainder, “Resume” continues and “Reset” returns the initial state. The calculator shows what time the timer will end and at the end a sound alert plays, which you can turn off. For the Pomodoro technique 25 minutes is convenient, i.e. 1,500 seconds.

The remainder in the tab title

While a countdown or timer runs, the remainder is shown in the browser tab title, so you can see it even when you work in another tab. When the timer finishes, the title changes to “Time is up!”. A browser may slow background pages down, but the remainder is calculated from the current time, so it does not fall behind.

Sharing a countdown

The “Share” button creates a link with the name, date, time and time zone of the event. Whoever opens it immediately sees the same live countdown, for example to a shared event or deadline. Timer settings are also kept in the link, but the timer itself does not start until you press “Start”.

Notes and limits

The counter counts real seconds between the current moment and the event, so on the night of the spring clock change a day in Kyiv has 23 hours and on the autumn change 25. The calculator relies on the device clock: if its time or zone is off, the result shifts too. Sound is enabled only after your click on “Start”, as browsers require. Dates from 1970 to 2100 are allowed.

The countdown follows your device clock: if it is off, the remainder will be off too. Keep the tab open.

FAQ

How do I make a countdown to a date online?

Enter the event date (and optionally the time and name): the countdown starts automatically and updates every second.

Does the countdown update without reloading?

Yes, the counter updates every second. It is calculated from the current time, so it does not fall behind even if the tab is in the background.

What happens when the event time arrives?

A message appears saying the event has arrived and the counter switches to counting “since the event”.

Can I pick a date in the past?

Yes, the calculator shows how long ago that event was.

How do I account for another time zone?

In the “Event time zone” field choose a city or UTC: the calculator shows both the event time and the same moment by your clock.

Is the daylight saving change accounted for?

Yes. The counter counts real seconds, so on the night of the change a day in Kyiv lasts 23 or 25 hours.

How do I start a 5-minute timer?

Open the “Timer” tab, press “5 min” and “Start”. You can also enter your own time up to 99 hours 59 minutes 59 seconds.

Does the timer have a pause?

Yes: “Pause” keeps the remainder, “Resume” continues and “Reset” returns the initial value.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers enable sound only after a user action, so start the timer with the “Start” button and check that the alert is on and the volume is not muted. The tab can be in the background.

Can I see the remainder in another tab?

Yes, the remainder is shown in the browser tab title, and when the timer finishes it changes to “Time is up!”.

How do I share a countdown?

Press “Share” and send the link: it restores the name, date, time and time zone of the event.

Why does the countdown not match my clock?

The calculator relies on the device clock. Check that the time, date and time zone on the device are set automatically.
