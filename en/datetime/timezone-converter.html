How the converter works

Choose a date, a time and the zone in which that time is given, plus the zone to convert to. The calculator shows the time in the second city, the weekday, the date with a “same / next / previous day” marker and the difference in hours. For example, 18:00 in Kyiv on November 6, 2026 is 11:00 in New York, a difference of 7 hours. The “Now” button fills in the current time in your zone and “Swap” reverses the direction.

Daylight saving time

The difference between cities changes during the year because countries switch clocks on different days. The calculator shows the offset of each zone for the chosen date, not for today. For example, Kyiv and New York are usually 7 hours apart, but on March 15, 2026 it is only 6 hours: the US has already moved to summer time (March 8) and Ukraine has not yet (March 29). So 18:00 in Kyiv is then 12:00 in New York.

Times that do not exist and times that repeat

When daylight saving time starts, an hour disappears: on March 29, 2026 in Kyiv the clocks go from 03:00 to 04:00 and 03:30 does not exist. The calculator warns you and shows the result for the shifted time. When it ends an hour repeats: on October 25, 2026, 03:30 in Kyiv happens twice. Then the first, daylight saving, occurrence is shown and the second one is described. The same applies to New York on March 8 and November 1.

Non-whole offsets

Not every zone differs by a whole hour. India uses UTC+5:30, Nepal UTC+5:45 and part of Australia also half an hour. The calculator takes such offsets into account and shows them in the result, for example 12:00 in Kyiv is 15:30 in Delhi.

Big cities by country

Besides capitals and zones, the list includes large cities of the USA, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, Germany, Italy, Brazil and Argentina: Houston, Manchester, Marseille, Barcelona, Munich, Milan, Rio de Janeiro, Rosario and many more. The country is shown next to the name. All cities of one country in one zone show the same time (for example all of London, Manchester and Edinburgh), but they are easier to find by name. The USA and Brazil have several zones, so Houston is on Central time and Seattle on Pacific time.

A meeting in several cities

The “Meeting in several cities” tab looks for a time that suits everyone. Enter the date, your zone, up to nine participant cities and the working hours (09:00–18:00 by default). The calculator checks the day in 15-minute steps and shows the common window and its start and end in every city. For Kyiv, London and New York on November 6, 2026 that is 16:00–18:00 in Kyiv, i.e. 14:00–16:00 in London and 09:00–11:00 in New York. For Kyiv and Tokyo the window is shorter: from 09:00 to 11:00 in Kyiv.

If there is no common window

When there are no hours that suit everyone, the calculator shows the best match: in how many cities it is working time for the longest stretch, and who is outside it. If you also give a meeting time, a table below the result shows what time it is in each city, the offset and the state: working, off hours or night.

Limits

The calculator takes time zone and daylight saving rules from your browser's database (IANA), so for far future dates they may change if countries change their rules. Dates from 1970 to 2100 are allowed. For flights, tickets and legally significant events also check the time in the official schedule.

FAQ

How do I convert time from one time zone to another?

Enter the date, the time and the zone where it is given, plus the target zone: the calculator shows the time there and the weekday. 18:00 in Kyiv on 11/06/2026 is 11:00 in New York.

Is daylight saving time taken into account?

Yes. The offset of each zone is taken for the chosen date from the browser's time zone database, so the difference can change during the year.

Why is the difference between Kyiv and New York sometimes 6 hours?

The US moves to summer time a few weeks before Ukraine. Between those dates (March 8 to 29 in 2026) the difference is 6 hours instead of 7.

What does “next day” mean?

In the second city the next date has already arrived, for example 23:00 in Kyiv is 06:00 the next day in Tokyo. There is also a “previous day” marker.

What if the time I entered does not exist?

When daylight saving time starts an hour disappears: the calculator warns you and shows the result for the shifted time, for example 03:30 in Kyiv on 03/29/2026 becomes 04:30.

What if a time happens twice?

When daylight saving time ends an hour occurs twice. The calculator shows the first, daylight saving, occurrence and tells you what the second would be.

Why is the difference in India not a whole hour?

India uses UTC+5:30 and Nepal UTC+5:45. The calculator accounts for such offsets: 12:00 in Kyiv is 15:30 in Delhi.

How do I find a time for a meeting in several cities?

Open the “Meeting in several cities” tab, give the date, your zone and the participants' cities: the calculator finds a common window of working hours.

Can I change the working hours?

Yes, the “Working hours from” and “until” fields set your own range; the default is 09:00–18:00.

What if there is no common window?

The calculator shows the best match: how many cities are in working hours and who is outside them.

Where does the calculator get zone rules?

From your browser's time zone database (IANA). For important flights and events check the time in the official schedule.

Is my own time zone in the list?

Kyiv is in the list, and by default the calculator uses your device's zone if it is among the more than 500 cities.
