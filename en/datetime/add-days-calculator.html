What the calculator counts

The calculator adds years, months, weeks and days to a date or subtracts them from it, and all the amounts can be combined: for example 1 year, 3 months and 10 days. The start date is today by default. January 15, 2025 plus 90 days is April 15, 2025, a Tuesday, and plus 1 year 3 months and 10 days is April 25, 2026, a Saturday. The result also shows the weekday, the ISO week number and the distance from today (“in 90 days” or “37 days ago”).

Month ends and leap years

Months have different numbers of days, so when the target month has no such day its last day is used. Adding 1 month to January 31, 2025 gives February 28, and for 2024 it is February 29. The same goes for years: February 29, 2024 plus 1 year is February 28, 2025, and plus 4 years is February 29, 2028. Years and months are added first and only then weeks and days, so the order is always the same.

The start date as day 1

Normally “in 30 days” means the start date is not counted: from January 1, 2025 that is January 31. But in deadlines and schedules the first day is often counted as day 1. Then choose “The start date is day 1”: 30 days from January 1 end on January 30, and a month from January 1 ends on January 31. For subtraction the shift is mirrored: the result is a day closer to the start date.

Subtracting days from a date

Choose “Subtract” and the calculator finds a date in the past: 30 days before March 10, 2025 is February 8, 2025, a Saturday. That tells you when a period started, what the date was a week or a month ago, or where to start a countdown.

The date after working days

The “Working days” tab finds the date after N working days, skipping weekends and holidays. From April 28, 2025 plus 10 working days is May 12 (a Monday, 14 calendar days, 4 weekend days skipped). If your holiday list has May 1 and 8, the result shifts to May 14 (a Wednesday): two more days skipped. The start date is not counted, which is how Excel's WORKDAY function works.

Weekends and holidays

By default the weekend is Saturday and Sunday, but you can choose “Sunday only”, “Friday and Saturday” or “No weekend”. State holidays are not built in because they change, including during martial law: enter your own list of dates separated by commas, spaces or new lines in the MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD format. A holiday that falls on a weekend is not skipped separately, and holidays outside the range are ignored, which the calculator tells you.

Quick buttons and the link

Below the fields there are buttons for 7, 14, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 months and 1 year: one click fills in the amount and calculates at once. The “Share” button creates a link with all your fields, for example with a holiday list for colleagues.

Limits

The calculation uses the Gregorian calendar: for dates before 1582 the result is nominal. The calculator does not know about transferred working days and has no built-in state holidays. Legal deadlines (power of attorney, warranty, visa) have their own counting rules, so check them against the law and documents and use the calculator as an aid. Dates from the year 1000 to 2999 are allowed.

FAQ

How do I find the date in N days?

Enter the start date (or leave today), type N in the “Days” field and keep “Add”. For 01/15/2025 and 90 days that is 04/15/2025.

How do I subtract days from a date?

Choose the “Subtract” operation: the calculator shows a past date. 30 days before 03/10/2025 is 02/08/2025.

Can I add years, months and days at once?

Yes, fill in several fields. Years and months are added first, then weeks and days: 01/15/2025 + 1 year 3 months 10 days = 04/25/2026.

What happens if I add a month to January 31?

February has no 31st, so its last day is used: February 28 (the 29th in a leap year).

How does the calculator treat February 29?

If you add years to February 29 and February has 28 days in the target year, February 28 is used: 02/29/2024 + 1 year = 02/28/2025.

What does “The start date is day 1” change?

In the normal mode the start date is not counted: +30 days from January 1 is January 31. In the “day 1” mode it counts as day 1 and the result is January 30.

How do I find the date after N working days?

Open the “Working days” tab, enter the number and, if needed, holidays. From 04/28/2025 plus 10 working days without holidays is 05/12/2025, and with holidays on May 1 and 8 it is 05/14/2025.

Is the start date counted among the working days?

Not by default, as in Excel's WORKDAY: the first working day is the one after the start date. To count it as the first, choose “The start date is day 1”.

Does the calculator have state holidays?

No, they change. Enter holidays as your own list of dates and the calculator will skip them.

What is the ISO week number?

It is the international week numbering: a week starts on Monday and the first week of the year contains January 4. December 30, 2024 already belongs to week 1 of 2025.

Can I enter a fractional number of days?

No, the calculator accepts only whole non-negative numbers and shows an error rather than silently rounding. Use the “Subtract” operation to go the other way.

Is the calculator suitable for legal deadlines?

Only as an aid: it shows the calendar date. Check the counting rules (from the next day, moving to a working day) against the law and documents.
