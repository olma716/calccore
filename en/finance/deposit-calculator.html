How deposit income is calculated

Interest accrues every month on the current amount: the rate ÷ 12. The bank withholds tax from the interest, and the rest is net interest. For a deposit of 100,000 UAH at 16% per year for 12 months with compounding the gross interest is 16,935.10 UAH, the 23% tax is 3,895.10 UAH, the net income is 13,040.00 UAH and at the end you receive 113,040.00 UAH. Interest and tax are rounded to the cent every month.

A top-up is added at the start of the month, from the second one. If you add 2,000 UAH every month, over 36 months you put in 170,000 UAH and the net income is 59,021 UAH instead of 44,443 UAH without top-ups.

Compounding, monthly payout or payout at the end

Compounding adds net interest to the deposit every month, so later interest is charged on a larger amount: over a year the net income is 13,040 UAH and the effective yield 13.04%. Monthly payout to the card keeps the deposit unchanged: net income 12,320 UAH (12.32%). Payout at the end is simple interest in one payment: net income 12,320 UAH.

So the same nominal rate gives different results depending on the payout terms. Compare deposits by the effective annual yield after tax that the calculator shows.

Tax and the real yield

By default the calculator uses a 23% tax (personal income tax 18% + military levy 5%) that the bank withholds from the interest. For the example above it is 3,895 UAH; under the old 19.5% rate the tax would have been 3,311 UAH. The rate is set by law and may change, so you can switch it off or set it by hand.

If you enter the expected inflation, the calculator shows the real yield. With an effective yield of 13.04% and 10% inflation per year it is about 2.76%. That is the user's assumption, not a forecast.

The calculation is an estimate. Accrual rules, rounding and the rate are set by the bank and the tax rate by law, so check the current terms with the bank.

FAQ

How is deposit income calculated?

Interest accrues every month on the current amount (the rate ÷ 12) and tax is withheld from it. For 100,000 UAH, 16% and 12 months with compounding the gross interest is 16,935.10 UAH, the tax 3,895.10 UAH and the net income 13,040.00 UAH.

What is interest compounding and how does it matter?

Compounding adds net interest to the deposit, so later interest is charged on a larger amount. Over a year that is 13,040 UAH against 12,320 UAH when interest is paid to the card; the effective yield is 13.04% against 12.32%.

How do monthly payout, compounding and payout at the end differ?

Compounding: the balance grows and at the end you receive 113,040 UAH. To the card: the 100,000 UAH deposit stays the same and 12,320 UAH of net interest arrives monthly. At the end: simple interest, net income of 12,320 UAH in one payment.

What is the tax on deposit interest?

The calculator uses 23% by default: personal income tax 18% and military levy 5%. The bank withholds it as a tax agent when interest is accrued. The rate is set by law and may change, so you can switch it off or set it by hand, and you should check the current value.

What is the effective annual yield?

It is an annual rate that takes compounding, tax and top-ups into account. For a 16% deposit over 12 months with compounding and 23% tax it is 13.04%, and before tax 17.23%. It is the convenient way to compare deposits with different terms.

How do top-ups affect income?

Every top-up increases the base for interest. With a monthly top-up of 2,000 UAH over 36 months you put in 170,000 UAH and the net income is 59,021 UAH against 44,443 UAH without top-ups.

How do I account for inflation and what is the real yield?

Enter the expected inflation in % per year: the calculator divides the effective yield by the price growth. With 13.04% and 10% inflation the real yield is about 2.76%. Inflation is your assumption, not a forecast.

How does accrual at 1/12 of the rate differ from accrual by days?

In the “1/12 of the rate” mode the same share accrues every month. By days, interest is the rate ÷ 365 × the number of days in the month. For 100,000 UAH, 16% and 12 months the net income is 13,040.00 UAH against 13,039.96 UAH. Your bank states which method it uses in the deposit terms.

Can I download the schedule to Excel?

Yes. The “Download CSV” button saves the table with unformatted numbers (a dot as the decimal separator, two decimals) that Excel opens as numbers. You can also copy the table and paste it into a sheet.

Why can the result differ from the bank?

It is an estimate from standard formulas. Banks may count days differently, round, pay interest on other dates and apply extra product terms. Deposits are protected by the deposit guarantee system up to a limit set by law, so check the terms and guarantees with the bank.
