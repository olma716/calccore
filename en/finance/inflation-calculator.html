How inflation between two dates is calculated

The calculator multiplies the monthly consumer price indices from the month after the start to the end month inclusive. The start month is the price base. For 100,000 UAH from January 2015 to September 2026 the total inflation is 308.3%, so to buy what 100,000 UAH bought in January 2015 you need 408,296 UAH today. The average annual inflation over the period is 12.8%.

The data is open statistics of the State Statistics Service of Ukraine for 2000–2026 (through the Minfin summary table). The highest annual inflation was recorded in 2015 (43.3%), and in 2022 it was 26.6%. There was deflation (falling prices over a year) in 2002 and 2012. From September 2025 to September 2026 prices rose by 10.0%.

A forecast at your own rate

The forecast mode calculates a constant inflation. With inflation of 10% per year, after 5 years 100,000 UAH kept as cash will have the purchasing power of 62,092 UAH, and to keep the value you need 161,051 UAH. The purchasing power of money halves in about 7.3 years at 10% inflation.

If you enter a deposit rate, the calculator shows the real yield: a deposit at 16% per year with monthly compounding (effectively 17.23%) with 10% inflation gives a real yield of about 6.6%. Compare it with the deposit calculator, which accounts for tax.

Prices and savings

Inflation erodes cash and money that is not invested. If a deposit yields less than inflation, the real yield is negative: the money grows in numbers but buys less. To judge the result compare not the nominal rate but the real yield after inflation.

The inflation in the calculator is the general consumer price index. Prices of particular goods (food, rent, fuel) may change differently, so for specific purchases the result is approximate.

The historical data is open statistics of the State Statistics Service of Ukraine (through the Minfin summary table) as of September 2026, updated on 22.10.2026. The forecast is an estimate at your constant rate: real inflation varies.

FAQ

How do I work out what money from past years is worth today?

Choose the “Historical inflation” mode and enter the amount and the start and end months. For 100,000 UAH from January 2015 to September 2026 you need 408,296 UAH to buy the same. The total inflation is 308.3%.

Where does the data on inflation in Ukraine come from?

The monthly consumer price indices of the State Statistics Service of Ukraine for 2000–2026, taken from the Minfin summary table (index.minfin.com.ua). For every full year the product of the 12 monthly indices matches the annual value. The data is updated as new months are published; the latest month in the calculator is September 2026.

What was the highest inflation in Ukraine?

In 2000–2025 the highest annual inflation was in 2015: 43.3% (December to December). In 2022 it was 26.6% and in 2014 24.9%.

Was there deflation in Ukraine?

Yes, in 2002 and 2012 the annual indices were below 100% (-0.6% in 2002, -0.2% in 2012). The calculator handles negative inflation correctly.

What is the inflation over the last year?

From September 2025 to September 2026 prices rose by 10.0%, and over 10 years since September 2016 by 176.0%. Use the quick buttons “1 year”, “5 years”, “10 years” for a fresh calculation.

How much value has money lost since the full-scale war began?

From February 2022 to September 2026 the total inflation is 69.4%. That means 100,000 UAH of February 2022 matches 169,359 UAH today in purchasing power.

How do I forecast the loss of the value of money?

Choose the “Forecast at a rate” mode and enter a constant inflation. At 10% per year 100,000 UAH in 5 years will have a purchasing power of 62,092 UAH (a loss of 37.9%). Real inflation varies, so this is an estimate.

What is the real yield of a deposit?

It is the yield after inflation: (1 + the effective rate) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1. A deposit at 16% per year with monthly compounding (effectively 17.23%) with 10% inflation gives about 6.6% of real yield. The calculator does not take the tax on interest into account.

Why is the start month not counted in inflation?

A month's index shows the change in prices compared with the previous month. So the price level of the start month is the base, and inflation is counted from the next month to the end month inclusive.

How accurate is the calculator?

The historical calculation relies on the official consumer price indices, so it is the general inflation, not a change in the price of a particular item. The forecast is an estimate at your rate. For important decisions check against the State Statistics Service data.
