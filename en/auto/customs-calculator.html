How the car customs calculator works

This customs calculator helps you quickly estimate import payments when bringing a car to Ukraine from Europe or the USA: duty, excise and VAT.

Enter the car price, delivery, year of manufacture and engine parameters — the calculator automatically determines the customs value and totals in UAH.

For electric vehicles, duty is often 0%, while excise is calculated from battery capacity (kWh). Since 1 January 2026 electric cars are again subject to 20% VAT, so the calculator does not apply a zero rate (you can change it in the “Duty and VAT rates” block if needed).

VAT when importing a car to Ukraine: what to know

VAT on car import is not charged only on the “car price”. The VAT base usually includes the customs value (price + delivery/insurance to border) plus duty and excise.

In this calculator VAT is calculated as: (customs value + duty + excise) × VAT rate.

Excise in Ukraine: what it depends on

Excise is a separate payment. For ICE vehicles it depends on fuel type, engine size and vehicle age: rate (€ per 1000 cc) × engine size in litres × age coefficient. The coefficient equals the number of years since manufacture, but is not less than 1 and not more than 15.

For electric vehicles excise is: 1€ × kWh.

Which vehicles can be calculated

Choose a category — passenger cars, trucks, motorcycles or buses — and an engine type: petrol, diesel, electric or hybrid. Passenger cars on petrol, diesel and electric use the built-in excise rates. For other categories and hybrids you enter the excise rate manually (you can find it in the Tax Code or ask a broker), while duty, VAT and the pension fee are editable.

Cars from the USA, Europe and other countries

The clearance formula is the same: duty + excise + VAT. The difference is in the customs value (price and delivery, usually in dollars from the USA and in euros from Europe) and in duty: for European-made cars with a certificate of origin the duty may be 0%, while for cars from the USA and other countries, and for non-European models bought in Europe, 10% usually applies.

Full cost of the car in Ukraine

Besides customs payments, the calculator shows what the car will cost “turnkey”: car price + duty + excise + VAT (and the pension fund fee) + your extra costs — broker, certification and registration at the service centre, compulsory insurance (OSAGO), delivery within Ukraine. For cars from the USA add the auction fee, delivery to the port and ocean freight — these amounts are part of the customs value. The result is shown in UAH and in the calculation currency, and the chart shows how much goes to duty, excise and VAT. For a more detailed estimate for cars from Europe use the total cost of a car from Europe calculator, and calculate insurance in the OSCPV calculator.

Comparing two options and the NBU rate

Save the calculation as option A, change the country, engine, year or price — and the calculator shows the difference between the options in customs payments and in the full cost (for example, USA vs Europe or petrol vs electric). Payments use today’s NBU rate; if needed, pick the rate for a specific date or enter your own USD or EUR rate. The car price can be entered in 12 currencies.

Pension fund fee

Besides customs payments, a pension fund fee is paid when registering a passenger car: 3% of the car value excluding VAT if it does not exceed 165 subsistence minimums for able-bodied persons, 4% between 165 and 290, and 5% above 290. In 2026 the subsistence minimum is UAH 3,328. Electric cars are exempt. In the calculator the fee is switched on separately and the subsistence minimum can be changed.

FAQ about car customs clearance

How does clearing a car from the USA, Europe or another country differ?

The formula is the same: duty + excise + VAT. The difference is in the customs value (price and delivery, usually in USD from the USA and EUR from Europe) and in duty: for European-made cars with a certificate of origin the duty may be 0%, while for cars from the USA and other countries 10% usually applies.

Which vehicle types can this calculator clear?

Passenger cars, trucks, motorcycles and buses with a petrol, diesel, electric or hybrid engine. Built-in excise rates exist for passenger cars (petrol, diesel, electric); for everything else you enter the excise rate manually.

What is included in customs clearance cost?

Duty, excise and VAT, plus the pension fund fee if needed and your extra costs for the full cost of the car. The table shows each payment in UAH and in the calculation currency at the NBU rate.

Is delivery included?

Yes. Delivery/insurance is added to the car price and forms the customs value that the payments are based on.

How is excise calculated?

For ICE: the rate per 1000 cc (depends on fuel and engine size) × engine size in litres × an age coefficient from 1 to 15. For EV — 1€ × kWh of battery.

How is VAT calculated on a car import?

VAT is calculated as (customs value + duty + excise) × VAT rate. The customs value usually includes the car price and delivery/insurance to the border.

Is the pension fund fee included?

Yes, if you turn on “Add the pension fund fee”. The rate of 3%, 4% or 5% depends on the car value (excluding VAT) in UAH compared with 165 and 290 subsistence minimums for able-bodied persons (in 2026 it is UAH 3,328, i.e. thresholds of UAH 549,120 and 965,120). The fee applies to passenger cars only, and electric cars are exempt.

Can I change the duty rate?

Yes. The default is 10% for ICE and 0% for EV. For European-origin cars tick “European-origin car (EUR.1 certificate)” — the duty becomes 0%. Other duty and VAT rates can be changed manually in the “Duty and VAT rates” block.

How do I calculate the full cost of a car in Ukraine?

Enter the car price and parameters, and in the “Extra costs in Ukraine” block add the broker, certification and registration, compulsory insurance and delivery. The calculator shows “The car in Ukraine will cost” — the price with all customs payments and expenses. For cars from the USA add the auction fee, delivery to the port and ocean freight.

How do I compare two car options?

Press “Save as option A”, change the data (for example, the country of origin or engine type) — a comparison with the B − A difference in customs payments and in the full cost appears below the result.

Which NBU rate is used for the payments?

The current NBU rate. In the “NBU rate: for a date or your own” block you can pick the rate for a specific date or enter your own USD or EUR rate — it takes priority.

How accurate is this calculator?

It gives an estimate based on the built-in rates and the NBU exchange rate. The final amounts are set by customs; check the details with a broker and current legislation.
