What the function grapher does

The calculator plots the graph of a function y = f(x) and lets you explore it: zoom, find zeros, extrema and intersection points, build a table of values, and find the derivative and the tangent line at a point. Up to six functions can be shown on one canvas, each has its own colour, and the dot next to a function switches it on or off.

How to enter functions

Write the expression as on paper: x^2 − 4x + 3, 2x + 1 (the multiplication sign can be omitted), 1/x, sqrt(x) or √x, abs(x) or |x|. The power is «^», for example x^3 or 2^x. Supported are sin, cos, tan (tg), cot, asin, acos, atan, sinh, cosh, tanh, ln, lg (log), log2, exp, sqrt, cbrt, abs, sign, floor, ceil, and the numbers π (pi) and e. Enter a vertical line as x = 3. In the settings you can choose degrees instead of radians.

Zeros, extrema and intersections

Zeros are the points where f(x) = 0, that is, the crossings with the x-axis. Extrema are local maxima and minima, where the derivative changes sign. For x² − 4x + 3 the zeros are 1 and 3, and the vertex (2; −1) is a minimum. The intersection points of two graphs solve f₁(x) = f₂(x): for x² − 4x + 3 and x − 1 they are x = 1 and x = 4. The calculator finds them numerically and suggests the exact form: a fraction, a multiple of π or √n.

Table of values

In the «Table of values» tab enter x from, to and a step, and all visible functions appear in columns. For polynomials and fractions with rational x the values are exact (for example 1/3), for other functions they use the chosen number of decimals. The table can be copied.

The derivative and the tangent

The derivative is found symbolically by the differentiation rules: (x³ − 3x)' = 3x² − 3. The tangent to the graph at x₀ is y = f(x₀) + f'(x₀)(x − x₀). For f(x) = x³ − 3x and x₀ = 2 we get f(2) = 2, f'(2) = 9, so the tangent is y = 9x − 16. The normal is the line perpendicular to the tangent at the same point, with slope −1/f'(x₀).

Typical graphs

A linear function y = kx + b is a line, k is the slope. A quadratic y = ax² + bx + c is a parabola with the vertex at x = −b/(2a). The hyperbola y = 1/x has asymptotes x = 0 and y = 0. The root y = √x is defined for x ≥ 0, the logarithm y = ln x for x > 0. Sine and cosine are periodic with period 2π, the tangent has vertical asymptotes at π/2 + πn. The exponential y = eˣ increases and never crosses the x-axis.

All calculations run in your browser. The expression is parsed by our own parser, and nothing is sent to a server.

FAQ

How do I plot a function online?

Enter the expression in the f₁(x) field, for example x^2 − 4x + 3, and the graph appears at once. To add another function, press «Add a function».

How do I find the zeros of a function?

Open the «Function analysis» tab or keep «Key points» on: zeros are shown as green dots. For x² − 4x + 3 they are 1 and 3.

How do I find the intersection of two graphs?

Enter both functions: the intersection points are marked in purple and listed under the graph. For x² − 4x + 3 and x − 1 they are x = 1 and x = 4.

How do I find an extremum?

An extremum is a point where the derivative changes sign. The calculator finds minima and maxima and shows them on the graph and in the «Function analysis» tab.

How do I find the tangent to a graph?

In the «Tangent» tab choose a function and a point x₀. The equation is y = f(x₀) + f'(x₀)(x − x₀). For x³ − 3x at x₀ = 2 it is y = 9x − 16.

How do I write a function with a root, absolute value or power?

sqrt(x) or √x, abs(x) or |x|, x^3, 2^x. The multiplication sign can be omitted: 2x, 3(x + 1).

Can I use degrees?

Yes: choose «Degrees» in the settings and sin(30) will equal 0.5. By default angles are in radians.

How do I change the scale?

With the mouse wheel, a two-finger pinch, a double click or the «+» and «−» buttons. You can also type the x and y limits.

How do I draw a vertical line?

Enter x = 3 (a number, not an expression with x): a vertical line at that point is drawn.

Why is the function missing at some points?

It is undefined there: for example 1/x at x = 0, ln x at x ≤ 0, √x at x < 0. The graph has a break there.

How do I save the graph?

The «Save PNG» button downloads the picture. The «Share link» button copies a link that restores the functions and the view.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run in your browser.
