What is a QR code

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode that's scanned by a camera and instantly decodes the embedded information: a link, text, contact details, and more. Unlike a regular barcode, a QR code can hold significantly more information thanks to its two-dimensional structure.

Practical uses of QR codes

  • Restaurant menus: a link to a digital menu instead of a paper one.
  • Business cards and ads: quick access to a website or portfolio without typing an address manually.
  • Payments: QR codes for fast transfers or bill payments.
  • Wi-Fi access: a QR code can contain data for automatically connecting to a network.
  • Product packaging: links to instructions, warranty info, or additional product details.

FAQ

Is it safe to generate a QR code online?

Yes, the QR code is generated entirely in your browser — the entered data is never sent to or stored on a server.

Does a QR code expire?

No, the QR code itself doesn't expire — it will always point to the entered address or display the entered text, as long as the linked content still exists.

Can I scan a QR code with a regular phone camera?

Yes, most modern smartphones recognize QR codes directly through the standard camera app, without needing a separate scanner.
