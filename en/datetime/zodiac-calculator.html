What the calculator shows

Enter your date of birth and the calculator finds your Western zodiac sign and your Chinese sign. For the Western one it shows the symbol, the element (fire, earth, air, water), the quality (cardinal, fixed, mutable), the ruling planet and the dates of the sign in that year. For the Chinese one it shows the animal, the element (wood, fire, earth, metal, water), yin or yang, and the start and end of the animal's year. For example, those born on February 10, 2026 are Aquarius in the Western zodiac and Snake in the Chinese one.

The Western zodiac and the position of the Sun

The Western (tropical) zodiac splits the year into 12 parts of 30° each from the March equinox, not by the constellations. So the sign depends on where the Sun is in the sky at that moment. The dates shift by about a day from year to year, and a fixed table like “Aries — March 21” gives errors. In 2026 the Sun enters Aries on March 20 at ≈ 14:45 UTC (≈ 16:45 Kyiv time), while in 2003 it happened on March 21 at ≈ 01:00 UTC.

The cusp of two signs

If you were born on the day the Sun moves into another sign, the calculator warns you and gives the time of the change. For example, on March 20, 2026 the Sun moves from Pisces to Aries at about 14:45 UTC: those born before that moment are Pisces, after it Aries. The sign is shown for Kyiv noon. For the exact sign you need the time and place of birth. The time of the change is approximate: for equinoxes and solstices the error is about 2 minutes, for the other signs up to 15 minutes.

Chinese New Year

The Chinese year of the animal does not begin on January 1 but at Chinese New Year, which falls between January 21 and February 20. So those born in January or early February often belong to the animal of the previous year. Chinese New Year 2026 fell on February 17, so those born on February 10, 2026 are still Snakes (the Snake year ran from January 29, 2025 to February 16, 2026), and from February 17 the Horse year begins, lasting until February 5, 2027. The calculator uses a table of Chinese New Year dates for 1900–2100.

Animals, elements and the cycle

The Chinese calendar combines 12 animals, 5 elements and yin or yang, so the full cycle lasts 60 years. The animal is (year − 4) mod 12 and the element comes from (year − 4) mod 10: an even value is yang, an odd one is yin. For 2026 that gives Horse and Fire (yang). The years of the Horse over recent decades are 1990 (metal), 2002 (water), 2014 (wood) and 2026 (fire).

The signs by dates for a chosen year

The “Signs by dates” tab shows for the chosen year when the Sun enters each of the 12 signs and the four astronomical events: the March equinox, the June solstice, the September equinox and the December solstice. In 2026 they are March 20, June 21, September 23 and December 21. The table helps you see how the dates of the signs shift from year to year.

Ruling planets

For each sign the ruling planet is shown. Traditional astrology used the seven visible bodies, so Scorpio belongs to Mars, Aquarius to Saturn and Pisces to Jupiter. Modern astrology added Pluto, Uranus and Neptune. The calculator shows both versions when they differ.

Limits

Dates from 1900 to 2100 are allowed. Astrology has no scientific support: it is an entertainment and cultural tradition, not a forecast of character or fate. The calculator determines the sign by the astronomical position of the Sun and by the calendar, without adding personality descriptions.

FAQ

How do I find my zodiac sign by date of birth?

Enter the date on the “My sign” tab: the calculator shows the Western sign by the position of the Sun and the Chinese animal. Those born on February 10, 2026 are Aquarius and Snake.

Why do the dates of the signs differ from tables elsewhere?

The Sun enters a sign at a different time every year, so the boundaries shift by about a day. The calculator uses the real moment of the change, not a fixed date.

What if I was born on the cusp of two signs?

The calculator warns you and shows the time the Sun changes sign. Those born before it belong to the previous sign, after it to the next. For the exact sign you need the time and place of birth.

Why am I in the previous year by the Chinese zodiac?

The Chinese year starts at Chinese New Year (between January 21 and February 20). Those born before it belong to the animal of the previous year.

When was Chinese New Year in my birth year?

The “Chinese New Year” tile on the “My sign” tab shows the date, and the “Chinese by years” tab gives all the years of an animal.

What do the element and yin/yang mean in the Chinese sign?

They are features of the 60-year cycle: the five elements (wood, fire, earth, metal, water) alternate every two years, and yin and yang alternate every year.

Which years belong to the Horse or another animal?

Open the “Chinese by years” tab and choose an animal. The years of the Horse are 1990, 2002, 2014, 2026.

How does the Western zodiac differ from the constellations?

The tropical zodiac is tied to the equinox and the seasons, not to the constellations. Because of precession the signs and the constellations of the same name have drifted apart by about a whole sign over the centuries.

How accurate is the time the Sun enters a sign?

About 2 minutes for equinoxes and solstices and up to 15 minutes for the other signs. That is why the time is rounded to 5 minutes and marked “≈”.

Does astrology have scientific support?

No. Astrology has no scientific support for any influence on character or fate: it is an entertainment and cultural tradition.

Why are there two kinds of ruling planets?

Traditional astrology used the seven visible planets, while Uranus, Neptune and Pluto, discovered later, became the modern rulers of Aquarius, Pisces and Scorpio.

Which years does the calculator support?

From 1900 to 2100 for both zodiacs. For the Chinese calendar the dates of New Year come from a table for these years.
