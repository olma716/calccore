How an operating lease payment is calculated

The monthly payment has two parts. Depreciation is the car price minus the down payment and the residual value, divided by the number of months. The finance part is interest on the average value of the car over the term: (price − down payment + residual) × rate ÷ 24 per month.

For a 800,000 UAH car, a 100,000 UAH down payment, a 50% residual value, 36 months and an 8% rate: depreciation is 8,333 UAH, the finance part 3,667 UAH, 12,000 UAH per month in total.

Financial lease with a buyout

In a financial lease you pay instalments that repay the value of the car plus interest (an annuity), and at the end you buy the car out at the buyout value. The payment is calculated with a final buyout payment B: (P − B ÷ (1+r)ⁿ) × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where P is the price without the down payment, r is the monthly rate and n is the term.

For the same car at a 12% rate and a 10% buyout the payment is 21,393 UAH per month plus a 80,000 UAH buyout at the end; 950,143 UAH in total with the down payment.

Lease or loan

To compare, enter the loan rate: the calculator computes the net cost of using the car: all payments (with the down payment, fees, insurance and buyout) minus the value of the car at the end for whoever keeps it. With an operating lease the car is returned, so its value at the end is not credited.

In the example with a 1% fee, 3% yearly insurance and a loan at 12%: the lease costs 612,000 UAH versus 537,001 UAH for the loan (assuming the car is worth 50% at the end).

What a lease includes besides the payment

The arrangement fee is usually a few percent of the price and is paid at the start. Comprehensive insurance is often mandatory for the whole term. VAT is added to the payments if the lessor invoices them with VAT (usually for VAT-registered companies). These amounts and rates are approximate and depend on the lessor, so check them in the contract.

The calculation is a simplified model. Real lease terms may include extra fees, insurance and other commissions; VAT and fees depend on the lessor and the client’s status. Check the full contract.

FAQ

How is the monthly operating lease payment calculated?

Payment = depreciation + finance part. Depreciation is (price − down payment − residual value) ÷ term, the finance part is (price − down payment + residual) × rate ÷ 24 per month. For the calculator example that is 8,333 + 3,667 = 12,000 UAH per month.

What is the residual value?

It is the forecast value of the car at the end of the lease, usually as a percentage of the price. The higher the residual value, the less the car loses during the term and the lower the payment: you pay only for the difference.

How does a financial lease with a buyout differ from an operating lease?

In an operating lease the car is returned to the lessor at the end and the payment covers only the difference down to the residual value. In a financial lease you buy the car out and pay an annuity, so the payment is higher but the car stays with you.

How is the finance part of the payment calculated?

Interest is charged on the average value of the car over the term: (price − down payment + residual) ÷ 2 × rate ÷ 12. That is the same as (price − down payment + residual) × rate ÷ 24 per month, or a money factor of rate ÷ 2400.

What extra costs does a lease have?

An arrangement fee (at the start), comprehensive insurance (spread over the term), VAT on payments (depending on the client’s status) and other one-time costs such as registration or valuation. You can enter all of them in the extra costs block.

Does the calculator include VAT?

Yes, optionally: enter a VAT rate and it is added to the payments and the buyout. For an individual VAT is usually already included in the car price, so you can leave the field empty. The exact terms depend on the lessor.

Lease or loan: which is cheaper?

Compare the net cost of using the car, not just the payment. In the example with a 1% fee, 3% insurance and a 12% loan the lease costs 612,000 UAH versus 537,001 UAH for the loan, but with a loan the car stays yours.

What is the net cost of using the car?

It is all your payments over the term (down payment, instalments, fee, insurance, buyout) minus the value of the car at the end if you keep it. It shows what using the car really costs, not just the sum of the payments.

Can I calculate a lease in dollars or euros?

Yes: choose the currency and all amounts are shown in it. The calculator does not convert currencies, so enter the price, the down payment and other amounts in one currency and use the rate that applies to it. Rates in dollars or euros are usually lower than in hryvnia.
