What car depreciation is

Depreciation is the loss of a car's market value over time because of age, mileage, wear and new models. In everyday speech it is often called amortization, although in accounting amortization means spreading an asset's cost into expenses by set rules, not the market price. The calculator shows market depreciation.

For a car worth 800,000 UAH the average profile gives 640,000 UAH after a year and 433,687 UAH after 5 years, a loss of 45.8% — about 6,105 UAH per month.

How the calculator works out the value

Every year the value is multiplied by a retention factor that depends on the car's age: the biggest drop is in the first year, then the pace slows. The benchmarks come from open US market data: on average a car loses about 46% in 5 years, electric cars about 59%, and the first year takes about 20%.

What affects depreciation

The biggest factors are the make and model, age, mileage, technical condition, engine type, trim and market demand. Niche and luxury cars and electric cars usually depreciate faster, while pickups, hybrids and popular SUVs hold their value better. Depreciation is reduced by regular servicing, no accidents and a full history.

This is a forecast from averaged data (USA: iSeeCars, LendingTree), not an appraisal of a particular car. The real value depends on the model, mileage, condition, trim and the market; to buy or sell, compare with listings.

FAQ

How much value does a car lose in the first year?

About 20% of its value, which is the largest drop over its life. For a car worth 800,000 UAH the average profile gives 640,000 UAH after a year, a loss of 160,000 UAH.

How much does a car lose in 5 years?

For an average car about 46% (iSeeCars data: 45.6% on average). In the 800,000 UAH example the value after 5 years is 433,687 UAH, a loss of 366,313 UAH.

How much does a car lose in 10 years?

By the average profile it keeps 42% of its original value, a loss of about 58%. For 800,000 UAH that is 335,532 UAH.

Which cars depreciate the fastest?

Luxury, niche and electric cars. With the “loses value fast” profile a car worth 800,000 UAH is worth 346,540 UAH after 5 years (a 57% loss), with the electric profile 329,270 UAH (a 59% loss).

Which cars hold their value best?

Popular models with strong demand: pickups, hybrids, Japanese SUVs. With the “holds its value” profile 61% remains after 5 years (486,419 UAH out of 800,000 UAH).

Do electric cars depreciate faster than petrol ones?

Yes. According to iSeeCars, electric cars lose about 59% in 5 years (57% in 2026) against about 46% for the average car. The reasons are fast battery progress, falling prices of new models and doubts about battery life.

How do I calculate depreciation for a car that is not new?

Enter the car's age now and the calculator starts the curve from the matching year. A three-year-old car worth 800,000 UAH loses 19.6% over the next 3 years and keeps 643,411 UAH.

How do I use my own depreciation percentage?

Choose “Own percentage” and enter a constant yearly depreciation. For example 12% a year over 5 years gives 422,186 UAH out of 800,000 UAH (a 47.2% loss).

What is the difference between depreciation and amortization?

Depreciation is the change in a car's market value. Accounting amortization spreads an asset's cost into expenses by set rules and does not equal the market price. This calculator works out market depreciation.

How is the residual value related to a lease?

In a lease the residual value is the share of the car's price that is not paid off in the payments but is left until the buyout. The calculator shows what percentage of the price remains after the chosen period and links to the lease calculator with that residual value filled in.
