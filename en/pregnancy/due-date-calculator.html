How the due date is calculated

The most common method is Naegele’s rule: 280 days (40 weeks) are added to the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP). For an LMP of January 1, 2026 the due date is October 8, 2026 (Thursday).

If the cycle is not 28 days, the date is adjusted: the difference between your cycle and 28 days is added. With a 35-day cycle the date moves 7 days later — October 15, 2026, and with a 24-day cycle 4 days earlier: October 4, 2026. The calculator accounts for the cycle length.

Other methods: conception, IVF, ultrasound

From the conception date 266 days (38 weeks) are added: conception on January 15, 2026 gives October 8, 2026. With IVF you count 266 days minus the age of the embryo from the transfer date: for a 5-day embryo a transfer on January 20, 2026 gives October 8, 2026, for a 3-day embryo October 10, 2026.

The ultrasound date is based on the age measured at the scan: if on March 12, 2026 the age was 9 weeks 3 days, the due date is October 12, 2026. An ultrasound before 13 weeks 6 days is considered the most accurate way to determine the gestational age.

Key dates of pregnancy

The second trimester starts at 14 weeks (April 9, 2026), the third at 28 weeks (July 16, 2026), full term from 37 weeks (September 17, 2026). Birth is most likely between 37 and 42 weeks: September 17 – October 22, 2026.

Maternity leave (a sick-leave certificate) is possible from 30 weeks — that is July 30, 2026: the leave lasts 70 days before the birth and 56 after (70 if the birth is complicated or there are several babies).

The calculator gives an estimate and does not replace a doctor’s consultation. The ultrasound redating thresholds follow ACOG; the examination windows and the start of maternity leave are general guidance, and your doctor sets the schedule.

FAQ

How do I calculate the due date?

Add 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the last menstrual period. For an LMP of January 1, 2026 it is October 8, 2026. The calculator also accounts for your cycle length and other methods: conception, IVF, ultrasound.

What is Naegele’s rule and how do I account for the cycle?

Naegele’s rule is the LMP + 280 days for a 28-day cycle. If the cycle is longer or shorter, add the difference: with 35 days the date is October 15, 2026 instead of October 8, 2026, with 24 days October 4, 2026.

How do I calculate the due date from conception?

Add 266 days (38 weeks) to the conception date. For conception on January 15, 2026 the due date is October 8, 2026.

How do I calculate the due date with IVF?

From the transfer date add 266 days minus the embryo age: +261 days for a 5-day embryo, +263 for a 3-day one. For a transfer on January 20, 2026 that is October 8, 2026 and October 10, 2026.

How accurate is the due date?

It is a guide: only about 4–5% of babies are born exactly on the calculated day. Most births happen between 37 and 42 weeks (September 17 – October 22, 2026 for an LMP of January 1, 2026).

What if the LMP and ultrasound dates differ?

The dating is usually revised by the ultrasound if the discrepancy exceeds 5 days up to 8 weeks 6 days, 7 days up to 15 weeks 6 days, 10 days up to 21 weeks 6 days, 14 days up to 27 weeks 6 days and 21 days later (ACOG guidance). The doctor makes the final decision.

When does maternity leave start?

The sick-leave certificate for pregnancy and childbirth is issued from 30 weeks — for an LMP of January 1, 2026 that is July 30, 2026. The leave lasts 70 days before the birth and 56 after (70 if the birth is complicated or there are several babies).

When do the trimesters start?

The first trimester lasts up to 13 weeks 6 days, the second starts at 14 weeks (April 9, 2026 for an LMP of January 1, 2026), the third at 28 weeks (July 16, 2026).

How long does pregnancy last?

The standard length is 280 days, or 40 weeks, from the first day of the last menstrual period; from conception about 266 days. A pregnancy is considered full-term from 37 weeks.

How many months of pregnancy is it?

Doctors count obstetric months of 4 weeks (28 days): 40 weeks is 10 obstetric months. In calendar months a pregnancy lasts about 9 and a half. The calculator shows the current obstetric month and how many full months have passed.

Does the calculator replace a doctor’s consultation?

No. It is an estimate for information. The due date and the schedule of examinations are set by the doctor who looks after the pregnancy.
