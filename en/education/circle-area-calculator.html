What the circle area calculator does

The calculator finds the area of a circle (strictly, of a disk) from the radius, diameter, circumference or area, as well as the area of a sector with the arc length, the chord and the segment, and the area of a ring. The answer is written exactly in terms of π, for example 25π, with a decimal approximation beside it. You can choose the value of π (exact, 3.14, 3.1416, 3.14159), the length unit and the number of decimal places, and the area is converted into other units too.

The formula for the area of a circle

S = πr², where r is the radius. The diameter d = 2r, so S = πd²/4. The circumference C = 2πr = πd, so r = C/(2π) and S = C²/(4π). If the area is known, r = √(S/π). For r = 5 we get S = π · 25 = 25π ≈ 78.5398.

Strictly speaking, a circle is a curve and a disk is the part of the plane inside it, so the area belongs to the disk. In everyday language and searches people say “circle area”, so the calculator is named that way.

Area from the diameter, circumference and area

If the diameter d = 10, the radius r = d / 2 = 5 and S = 25π. If the circumference C = 10π, then r = C / (2π) = 5. If the area S = 25π, then r² = 25 and r = 5. The calculator accepts C and S in the form of π (10π, 25π), and then the radius comes out exactly, while for plain numbers (for example C = 31.4159) the radius is found approximately.

Sector and arc

A sector is the part of a disk between two radii. For an angle α in radians the arc length is l = rα and the sector area is S = r²α/2. In degrees, α radians = α · π/180, that is S = πr² · α/360°. For r = 6 and α = 60° we get l = 2π ≈ 6.2832 and S = 6π ≈ 18.8496. The chord c = 2r · sin(α/2) = 6, and the segment area r²(α − sin α)/2 ≈ 3.2611.

Ring

A ring is the part of the plane between two concentric circles with radii R and r. Its area is S = π(R² − r²) = π(R − r)(R + r), and its width is w = R − r. For R = 5 and r = 3 we get S = π · (25 − 9) = 16π ≈ 50.2655, with a width of 2.

The value of π and units

At school π ≈ 3.14 is often used, so 25π gives 78.5, while the exact π gives 78.5398. The calculator shows both if you change the value of π in the settings. Area is measured in square units: cm², m², in² and so on. The conversion is exact: 1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 ft = 304.8 mm, 1 ha = 10 000 m².

All entered numbers are handled exactly (fractions are not rounded); π is substituted only for the decimal approximation.

FAQ

How do I find the area of a circle?

The area is π times the square of the radius: S = πr². For r = 5 it is 25π ≈ 78.5398.

How do I find the area from the diameter?

First r = d / 2, then S = πr². For d = 10: r = 5, S = 25π. Or directly S = πd²/4.

How do I find the area from the circumference?

r = C / (2π), then S = πr². You can also use S = C² / (4π) directly. For C = 10π the radius is 5 and the area is 25π.

How do I find the radius from the area?

r = √(S / π). For S = 25π the radius is 5.

What is the difference between a circle and a disk?

A circle is a curve (the boundary), a disk is the whole region inside it. The area belongs to the disk and the length to the circle. In everyday use they are often not distinguished.

Should I use 3.14 or the exact π?

It depends on the task. School problems usually take π ≈ 3.14, precise calculations use more digits. In the calculator the value of π is chosen in the settings.

How do I find the area of a sector?

S = πr² · α / 360°, where α is the angle in degrees. For r = 6 and α = 60° the area is 6π ≈ 18.8496.

How do I find the arc length?

l = πr · α / 180°, or l = rα if the angle is in radians. For r = 6 and α = 60° the length is 2π ≈ 6.2832.

What is a segment of a disk?

The part of the disk between an arc and its chord. Its area is r²(α − sin α)/2 with α in radians.

How do I find the area of a ring?

S = π(R² − r²), where R is the outer and r the inner radius. For R = 5 and r = 3 it is 16π ≈ 50.2655.

In which units is the area given?

In square units of the length you chose: cm → cm², m → m². The calculator also shows the area in other units.

Can I enter π in the input?

For the circumference, the area and an angle in radians you can write π: 10π, 25π, π/3. The result is then exact, without rounding.
