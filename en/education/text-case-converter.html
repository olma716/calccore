What the tool does

The “Case” tab turns text into UPPERCASE, lowercase, “sentence case” (only the first letter of each sentence is capital), “Title Case” (each word capitalised) or inverts the case. Other options give programmer styles: camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE. The “Cleanup” tab removes extra spaces, empty and duplicate lines, joins lines into one paragraph, replaces straight quotes with curly ones and a hyphen between spaces with a dash, removes digits or punctuation and sorts lines alphabetically.

Case for Cyrillic and Latin

The conversion works with any language, Ukrainian included: “hello, world” → “HELLO, WORLD”, and Cyrillic text is converted just as well. Sentence case turns “first line. SECOND line!” into “First line. Second line!”. Note that all other letters become lowercase, so proper names, abbreviations and names inside a sentence must be corrected by hand; Title Case likewise capitalises function words (“Is It Right”).

camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case

These styles turn a phrase into an identifier of a variable, function, file or address. From “Hello big world” you get helloBigWorld (camelCase), HelloBigWorld (PascalCase), hello_big_world (snake_case), hello-big-world (kebab-case) and HELLO_BIG_WORLD (CONSTANT_CASE). The text is first split into words (letters and digits), other characters are dropped and digits stay in place: “Hello big world 2024” → helloBigWorld2024.

Cleanup: what each action does

Trim spaces at line ends removes the indentation on both sides. Multiple spaces → one collapses runs of spaces and tabs. Spaces around punctuation fixes “text ,next .” to “text, next.”. Remove empty lines and duplicate lines are handy for lists, and Join lines for text copied from a PDF where every line is broken. The actions run from top to bottom in the order of the buttons, and the solution steps show how many characters each stage removed.

Cleanup example

The line “ This is a test text ,with a lot of extra spaces .” after “Trim”, “Multiple spaces → one” and “Spaces around punctuation” becomes “This is a test text, with a lot of extra spaces.”. A repeated line stays once after “Remove duplicate lines”, and the empty lines disappear.

Who needs it

The tool helps editors and copywriters to bring text to one form, students to format their work, developers to turn names into identifiers and marketers to prepare keyword lists without duplicates. All transformations run in your browser and the text is not sent anywhere; the result can be copied with one button.

“Sentence case” capitalises only the start of each sentence and lowercases the rest, so proper names in the middle of a sentence have to be fixed by hand.

FAQ

How do I make all letters capital?

On the “Case” tab choose “UPPERCASE” — the text changes instantly. For lowercase choose “lowercase”.

What does “Sentence case” mean?

A capital only at the start of each sentence and lowercase for the rest. Handy when text was typed with Caps Lock by accident.

How does Title Case differ from sentence case?

Title Case capitalises every word, including function words. Sentence case capitalises only the first word of each sentence.

What are camelCase and snake_case?

Programming styles for compound names: helloBigWorld has no spaces and capitals inside, hello_big_world uses underscores.

How do I remove extra spaces?

On the “Cleanup” tab turn on “Multiple spaces → one” and “Trim spaces at line ends”.

How do I delete empty lines?

Turn on “Remove empty lines”: all lines without visible characters disappear and the rest keep their order.

How do I remove duplicate lines?

Turn on “Remove duplicate lines”: only the first occurrence of each line stays (case is ignored).

How do I turn straight quotes into curly ones?

Turn on the corresponding action: opening quotes become “ and closing ones ”.

Can I combine several actions?

Yes, select several buttons on the “Cleanup” tab: they run from top to bottom in the order of the list.

Does it work with Cyrillic?

Yes, case changes for all languages, Ukrainian included; special Ukrainian letters are processed like any others.

Is my text stored?

No, the processing happens in your browser and the text is not sent to a server. The “Share link” contains only a small fragment.

Why did names lose capitals in sentence case?

The mode lowercases everything except the first letter of a sentence. Proper names and abbreviations must be fixed by hand, or use UPPERCASE or Title Case.
