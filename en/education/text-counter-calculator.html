What the character and word counter does

The counter shows characters with and without spaces, letters, digits, words, unique words, sentences, paragraphs and lines, plus the average word length and the longest word. The “Count” tab gives all the statistics and estimates reading and speaking time, the “Limits and SMS” tab compares the length with platform limits, and “Word frequency” finds the words that repeat most. The sample text of 33 words and 206 characters gives 3 sentences and 2 paragraphs.

How characters, words and sentences are counted

Every Unicode character, emoji included, counts as one. Characters “with spaces” include spaces and line breaks, “without spaces” only visible symbols. A word is a sequence of letters and digits; an apostrophe or hyphen inside does not split it, so “don't” and “well-known” are one word each. A sentence ends with a full stop, exclamation or question mark or an ellipsis, and paragraphs are separated by an empty line. The result may differ from Word or Google Docs by a few words because programs treat dashes and abbreviations differently.

Reading and speaking time

Silent reading time is the number of words divided by the reading speed. An average adult reads about 200 words per minute, and a public speech runs at about 130 words per minute. These are guidelines: a complex scientific text is read slower, fiction faster. You can change both speeds on the “Count” tab and the times are recalculated. 1,000 words at 200 words per minute take 5 minutes to read, while saying the same 1,000 words takes about 7 minutes 42 seconds.

Platform and SMS limits

The “Limits and SMS” tab shows whether the text fits 280 characters on X (Twitter), 2,200 characters of an Instagram caption, 4,096 characters of a Telegram message, 100 characters of a YouTube title and the usual SEO ranges: about 60 characters for a title and 160 for a description. For SMS it detects the encoding: Latin letters and basic symbols use GSM-7 (160 characters in one SMS, 153 per part of a longer one), while Cyrillic and emoji need UCS-2 (70 and 67 characters). Platform limits change from time to time, so treat them as guidelines.

Word frequency and lexical diversity

The “Word frequency” tab ignores case, filters out short function words by minimum length and shows the top words with their counts and share of all words. It helps to find filler words, check keyword density in an SEO text and judge vocabulary variety: the ratio of distinct words to all words. In the sample about language, the word “language” appears 5 times and “culture” 4 times.

Who needs it

The counter is useful to copywriters and editors (length requirements in characters), students (essays and abstracts), social-media managers (post limits), SEO specialists (title and description length) and anyone preparing a speech or a podcast. Everything runs in your browser: the text is never sent anywhere, and the short “Share link” stores only a small fragment of the text.

FAQ

How many characters are in my text?

Paste the text on the “Count” tab: the calculator shows characters with and without spaces, letters, digits and spaces separately.

What is the difference between characters with and without spaces?

“With spaces” includes spaces and line breaks, “without spaces” only visible symbols. Length requirements usually say which one is meant.

How is a word counted?

A word is a sequence of letters and digits. An apostrophe or a hyphen inside does not split it: “don't” and “well-known” are one word each.

How do I calculate reading time?

Divide the number of words by the reading speed. At 200 words per minute a text of 1,000 words takes 5 minutes. You can change the speed in the field.

How many words can you say in a minute?

About 130 words per minute for a speech and up to 150–160 for quick conversation. The value in the calculator is approximate; change it to suit you.

What is the character limit on X (Twitter)?

280 characters in a regular post. The “Limits and SMS” tab shows how many characters are left or by how many you exceed the limit.

How many characters fit in one SMS?

160 characters if the text is Latin letters and basic symbols (GSM-7), and 70 if it contains Cyrillic or emoji (UCS-2).

Why are SMS in Ukrainian shorter?

Cyrillic is not part of the GSM-7 set, so the message is encoded in UCS-2, which allows only 70 characters per SMS and 67 per part of a longer message.

What is the best title and description length?

For Google, roughly up to 60 characters for a title and up to 160 for a description: longer ones may be cut in search results. It is a guideline, not a hard limit.

Are emoji counted?

Yes, each emoji counts as one Unicode character, but in SMS and some networks it takes more room — the calculator accounts for this for SMS.

Is my text stored?

No. The counting happens in your browser and the text is not sent to a server. The “Share link” stores only a small fragment of the text in its address.

Why does the result differ from Word?

Programs count words differently, for example with dashes, hyphenated digits or abbreviations. The difference is usually a few words per thousand.
