What the calculator does

The calculator builds a wedding cost estimate by item: the banquet, venue, decor and flowers, music and host, photo and video, attire, beauty, cake, rings, ceremony, invitations, transport and your own items. A reserve is added to the sum, and the result shows the total cost, the cost per guest, the share of each item and how much is left to pay after the advances already made.

The banquet, children and per-guest costs

The banquet is counted for each adult guest, while children are charged at a percentage of the adult portion: 50% by default, but you can set your own. You can also add a cost for every guest, such as favours or a transfer. This removes the typical mistake of multiplying the cost per guest by everyone equally without allowing for children.

A reserve for the unexpected

The reserve is worked out on the sum of all items. Advice usually suggests setting aside about 5–10%: prices rise, guests are added and small expenses appear. In this calculator you can enter 0 and set no reserve at all: zero is no longer replaced by a default value, as it was on the old page.

Splitting a budget by percentages

If the total is already known, on the “Budget split” tab set the percentages by item or choose one of three layouts: a rule of thumb, more on the banquet or more on photo and decor. The calculator shows the amounts by item and per guest. When the percentages add up to exactly 100, the cents are split by the largest remainder method, so the sum equals the budget exactly.

How many guests a budget holds

The “How many guests” tab answers the reverse question: from the budget, the fixed costs, the cost per guest and the reserve it finds the largest number of guests and shows what the estimate would be with other guest numbers. If the budget does not hold even the fixed costs, the calculator says what is missing.

Rules of thumb from articles, not a norm

In advice on wedding budgets the banquet and venue together are usually put at 40–50% of the costs, photo and video at 10–12%, decor and flowers at 8–10%, music and entertainment at 5–10% and attire at 5–8%. This is mostly American advice, so your real shares may differ. The calculator's starting percentages are a rounded rule of thumb to adjust to your prices.

Limits

The calculator does not know prices in your city and suggests nothing for you: you enter all the amounts. Currencies are not converted and the symbol is chosen only for display. The maximum is 5000 guests, up to 30 own items and amounts up to a billion.

Set aside a reserve of at least 5–10% and fix the biggest items (the banquet and the venue) first: they decide the rest. Recalculate the estimate whenever the number of guests changes.

FAQ

How much does a wedding cost?

It depends on the country, city, season, venue and number of guests, so there is no single figure. Enter your prices for the banquet, venue and other items and the calculator builds the estimate.

What reserve should I set aside?

Advice usually names 5–10% of the sum of all items. The calculator defaults to 10%, but you can enter any value, including 0.

How is the banquet with children counted?

Adults are charged in full and children at a percentage of the adult portion, 50% by default. You can change the percentage in the “More items and guests” block.

What are costs per guest?

They are amounts multiplied by all the guests, children included: favours, a transfer, seating cards. They are added separately from the banquet.

How do I add my own costs that are not in the list?

In the “More items and guests” block paste lines “name amount”, for example “Fire show 500”. Write the amount without spaces. Up to 30 lines.

How do I find out how much is left to pay?

In the “Payment and currency” block enter the amount already paid: the calculator shows the amount left to pay or the overpayment.

What does the “Budget split” tab do?

From the total and the percentages it shows how much to set aside for each item and per guest. There are three ready layouts and the percentages can be changed.

Why should the percentages add up to 100?

Then the whole budget is split and the cents are divided with no remainder. If the sum is not 100, the calculator shows the unallocated remainder or the excess.

How do I find out how many guests I can invite?

On the “How many guests” tab enter the budget, the cost per guest, the fixed costs and the reserve. The calculator finds the maximum and shows the cost with other guest numbers.

Where do the rule-of-thumb percentages come from?

From advice on wedding budgets (Joy, WeddingWire, GiftList), mostly American: banquet and venue 40–50%, photo 10–12%, decor 8–10%. It is a guide, not a norm.

Can I share the estimate?

Yes, the “Share” button copies a link with the entered data. Remember the amounts will be visible to anyone who opens the link.

Is my data stored or sent?

No, everything is worked out in your browser and nothing is sent. The data goes into the link only when you press “Share” yourself.
