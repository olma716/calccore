What is running pace

Running pace is the time it takes to run 1 kilometer, expressed in minutes and seconds (e.g., 5:30 min/km). It's the main metric runners use, more convenient than speed (km/h) for planning training and races.

Three ways to use this calculator

  • Calculate finish time: knowing the race distance and your usual pace, find your estimated finish time.
  • Calculate pace: if you have a target time for a distance, find out what pace you need to run.
  • Calculate distance: knowing your pace and running time, find the approximate distance covered.

Predictions for standard distances

The calculator also shows estimated times for popular race distances (5K, 10K, half marathon 21.1K, marathon 42.195K) based on your entered pace. This is useful for planning goals for upcoming races.

These predictions are estimates — over longer distances, pace typically slows down due to accumulated fatigue, so your actual marathon finish time may be somewhat higher than a simple extrapolation from a shorter run's pace.

FAQ

What is running pace?

Running pace is the time it takes to run 1 kilometer (or mile), measured in minutes per kilometer (min/km).

How do I calculate marathon finish time from my current pace?

Multiply your pace (min/km) by the marathon distance (42.195 km). Keep in mind that pace usually slows slightly over longer distances compared to shorter runs.

Why will my marathon pace be slower than my 5K pace?

Over longer distances, the body has to sustain effort for much longer, so it's physiologically impossible to maintain the same pace as a shorter run.
