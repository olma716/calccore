What the rebar calculator counts

The calculator works out how much rebar is needed for several elements (up to six): a slab with a one or two layer mesh and the frame of a strip, a beam or a column with main bars and stirrups. For every element you enter the dimensions, the diameters, the steps and the cover, and also the length of the bars on sale, the lap and the reserve. The result is the length, the weight, the number of bars with laps and offcuts and, if you wish, tie wire and the cost from your prices in hryvnia, dollars or euros.

How rebar is counted for a slab and a frame

Slab: the length of the bars is the size of the slab minus two covers, and the number of bars in each direction is the size across divided by the step, plus one. For a slab of 6×4 m with a 20 cm step and no cover that is 21 bars of 6 m and 31 of 4 m, 250 m in total, 222 kg at Ø12 (9.3 kg per m²).

Frame: the main bars have the length of the element, and the stirrups are set at your step, their number is the length divided by the step, plus one. The perimeter of a stirrup is two section dimensions minus the covers, with no hooks. For a 20 m strip of a 40×80 cm section, 4 bars of Ø14 and Ø8 stirrups at 20 cm with a 5 cm cover that is 101 stirrups of 2.00 m.

Bars, lap and offcuts

Rebar is sold in bars of a standard length, most often 11.7 m. If a piece is longer, it is made of several bars with a lap. The lap is set in diameters and taken from the project. For a 20 m piece of Ø12 and a lap of 40 diameters (0.48 m) 2 bars are needed, the second segment is 8.78 m. The calculator cuts the segments from the longest to the shortest: each goes into the bar with the smallest leftover where it fits, otherwise a new bar is taken. So the number of bars is a little more than the length divided by the length of a bar.

Worked example

A slab of 6×4 m, Ø12 at a 20 cm step in two layers with a 3 cm cover, and a 20 m strip of a 40×80 cm section, 4 bars of Ø14, Ø8 stirrups at 20 cm, a 5 cm cover. A bar of 11.7 m, a lap of 40 diameters, a 5% reserve. The length with laps is 758.2 m, the weight by the calculation 0.600 t, 79 bars, 85 with the reserve, the weight bought 0.785 t. Wire at 2 kg per tonne gives 1.2 kg. At 50,400 ₴ per tonne and 80 ₴ per kg of wire the cost is 39,637 ₴.

The calculator gives an approximate estimate. The diameters, steps, lap and cover are set by the design of the structure.

FAQ

How do I calculate rebar for a slab?

The length of the bars is the size of the slab minus the covers, the number in each direction is the size across divided by the step, plus one. For a slab of 6×4 m with a 20 cm step and Ø12 in one layer that is 250 m and 222 kg.

How much rebar does a strip foundation need?

The main bars are the length of the strip times their number, the stirrups are counted by the step. For a 20 m strip of a 40×80 cm section, 4 bars of Ø14 and Ø8 stirrups at 20 cm with a 5 cm cover that is 101 stirrups and 282.0 m of rebar.

What is the lap and how is it counted?

The lap is the length by which two bars overlap when joined in one line. It is set in bar diameters and taken from the project. If the field is empty, the lap is not counted.

Why are there more bars than the length divided by the bar length?

Because the segments are cut from whole bars, and the end that is shorter than a segment goes to offcuts. Also, pieces longer than a bar are made of several bars with a lap.

Which rebar diameter and step should I choose?

It is set by the design of the structure for its loads. The calculator does not choose the diameter and the step, it counts by the values you enter.

How much does a metre of rebar weigh?

The weight of a metre is 0.00617 × d² kg, where d is the diameter in mm: Ø6 0.222, Ø8 0.395, Ø10 0.617, Ø12 0.888, Ø14 1.209, Ø16 1.580 kg/m.

What is the cover?

The layer of concrete between the rebar and the surface of the element. The calculator shortens the bars of a slab by two covers and reduces the perimeter of a stirrup. The value comes from the project, the field is empty by default.

How are stirrups counted?

The length of the element is divided by the stirrup step and one is added. The perimeter of a stirrup is two section dimensions minus the covers.

Does the calculator account for the hooks of stirrups?

No. Hooks make a real stirrup longer, so for accuracy add a reserve or increase the length of the element.

How much tie wire is needed?

The rate depends on the way of tying and the density of the mesh, so you enter it in kg per tonne of rebar. There is no default value.

How do I calculate the cost of rebar?

Per tonne it is the weight bought times the price, per bar the number of bars with the reserve, per metre the length of the bars bought. Wire and delivery are added separately.

What length are rebar bars?

The most common is 11.7 m, but suppliers have other lengths too, so the field “Bar length” can be changed.
