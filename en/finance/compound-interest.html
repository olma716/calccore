How compound interest works

Compound interest is charged not only on the amount you put in but also on the interest accrued before. For a deposit of 100,000 UAH at 10% per year with monthly compounding, after 10 years there will be 270,704 UAH, that is 170,704 UAH of income. Simple interest would give only 200,000 UAH.

The formula without contributions: S = P × (1 + r/n)n·t, where P is the initial amount, r the annual rate, n the number of compoundings per year and t the years. With regular contributions the calculator works month by month: a contribution earns interest from the month it is made.

Compounding frequency and contributions

The more often it compounds, the bigger the amount, but the effect fades. For 100,000 UAH at 10% over 10 years: yearly — 259,374 UAH, semi-annually — 265,330 UAH, quarterly — 268,506 UAH, monthly — 270,704 UAH, daily — 271,791 UAH.

Regular contributions speed up growth more than frequency: a monthly 2,000 UAH on top of the same 100,000 UAH gives 683,808 UAH instead of 270,704 UAH. And starting from zero, contributions of 2,000 UAH a month alone give 413,104 UAH in 10 years.

Tax, inflation and a goal

With a 23% tax on interest (as on a deposit) the result for the same example is 215,446 UAH instead of 270,704 UAH; the effective yield before tax is 10.47%. With 8% inflation per year, 270,704 UAH in 10 years has a real value of about 125,388 UAH in today's money.

The goal modes answer the reverse questions. To save 1,000,000 UAH from 100,000 UAH with contributions of 5,000 UAH a month at 10% you need 8 yr 4 mo. And to save 1,000,000 UAH in 10 years from zero you need to contribute about 4,843 UAH a month.

This is an estimate from a mathematical model. Real interest and rounding are set by the bank or the broker, investment returns are not guaranteed and the tax rate is set by law — check the current terms.

FAQ

What is compound interest?

Interest charged on the amount together with the interest accrued before. For 100,000 UAH at 10% over 10 years with monthly compounding you get 270,704 UAH, while simple interest would give 200,000 UAH.

What is the compound interest formula?

S = P × (1 + r/n)^(n·t), where P is the initial amount, r the annual rate, n the compoundings per year and t the years. With regular contributions the calculator works month by month and takes the moment of every contribution into account.

How does compounding frequency affect the result?

The more often it compounds the more you get, but the difference is small. For 100,000 UAH, 10% and 10 years: yearly 259,374, quarterly 268,506, monthly 270,704, daily 271,791 UAH.

What matters more: a higher rate or regular contributions?

At the start contributions matter more, later the rate and the time. A monthly 2,000 UAH on top of 100,000 UAH gives 683,808 UAH against 270,704 UAH without contributions. Compare the options in the calculator.

What is the rule of 72?

An approximate doubling time = 72 ÷ the rate in %. At 10% it is 7.2 years. The calculator also shows a more precise time based on the effective yield: for 10% with monthly compounding about 7.0 years.

How do compound and simple interest differ?

Simple interest is charged only on the initial amount, compound interest also on the accumulated interest. After 10 years at 10% that is 200,000 UAH against 270,704 UAH. The calculator shows the “gain from compounding” as the difference.

How do I include the tax on interest?

Choose “23%” (as on a deposit: personal income tax 18% and military levy 5%) or enter your own rate. The tax is withheld from the interest at compounding. For the example above the result is 215,446 UAH instead of 270,704 UAH. The rate is set by law, check the current one.

How do I account for inflation?

Enter the expected inflation in % per year: the calculator shows the real value in today's money. With 8% inflation, 270,704 UAH in 10 years is about 125,388 UAH in today's money. Inflation is your assumption.

How do I work out how long it takes to reach a goal?

Choose the “Time to a goal” mode and enter the initial amount, the contribution, the rate and the target. From 100,000 UAH with contributions of 5,000 UAH a month at 10% a million is saved in 8 yr 4 mo.

How much do I need to contribute each month for a goal?

Choose the “Contribution for a goal” mode: enter the goal, the rate and the term. To save 1,000,000 UAH in 10 years from zero at 10% with monthly compounding you need about 4,843 UAH a month.
