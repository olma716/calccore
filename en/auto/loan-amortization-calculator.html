How an annuity payment is calculated

An annuity payment is the same every month: P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual ÷ 12) and n is the number of months. For a 700,000 UAH loan at 12% for 36 months the payment is 23,250.02 UAH and the overpayment is 137,001 UAH. At the start most of the payment goes to interest, at the end to principal.

All amounts in the schedule are rounded to the cent and the last payment closes the balance exactly to zero.

Annuity or differentiated payments

In a differentiated schedule the principal is the same every month and the interest falls with the balance. For the same 700,000 UAH, 12% and 36 months the payment falls from 26,444.44 UAH to 19,639.05 UAH and the overpayment is 129,500 UAH. That is 7,501 UAH less than with an annuity (137,001 UAH), but the first payments are noticeably larger.

Early repayment

An extra payment goes entirely to principal, so interest is charged on a smaller balance. If in the example you pay an extra 5,000 UAH every month, the loan closes in 29 months instead of 36 and the interest savings are 28,571 UAH. The payment itself does not change.

Real annual rate

The real annual rate (APR) counts not only interest but also the fee and insurance. The calculator estimates it as the internal rate of return of the cash flow (the loan received minus the fee, then payments plus insurance) multiplied by 12. In the example with a 1% fee and 1% yearly insurance it is about 14.44% with a nominal rate of 12%. It is an estimate: the exact value and formula are stated by the bank in the agreement.

The calculation is an estimate. Real loan terms, fees, insurance and the real rate are set by the bank, so check them in the loan agreement. The real annual rate here is approximate.

FAQ

What is a loan amortization schedule?

It is a table showing how each payment is split into interest and principal and how the balance falls month by month. The calculator shows it by month and by year with totals, and you can copy it or download a CSV for Excel.

How is the monthly annuity payment calculated?

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)⁻ⁿ), where r is the monthly rate. For 700,000 UAH, 12% and 36 months it is 23,250.02 UAH and the total overpayment is 137,001 UAH.

How do differentiated payments differ from annuity ones?

A differentiated payment falls: the principal is the same and the interest shrinks. For the same terms it goes from 26,444.44 to 19,639.05 UAH with an overpayment of 129,500 UAH. An annuity gives a constant 23,250.02 UAH payment and a larger overpayment.

Why is there more interest than principal at the start of a loan?

Interest is charged on the outstanding balance, which is the largest at the start. With every payment the balance falls, so the share of interest drops and the share of principal grows.

How does early repayment reduce the overpayment?

An extra payment reduces the balance, so interest is charged on a smaller amount. In the example an extra 5,000 UAH per month shortens the loan to 29 months and saves 28,571 UAH of interest.

What is the real annual rate and how does it differ from the nominal one?

The nominal rate counts interest only, while the real one also counts fees and insurance. In the example with a 1% fee and 1% yearly insurance it is about 14.44% instead of 12%. The calculator gives an estimate, the exact rate is stated by the bank.

How do I include a fee and insurance?

Enter the arrangement fee as a percentage of the loan (paid once at the start) and insurance as a percentage of the loan per year (spread over the months). They increase the total cost of credit and the real annual rate.

Can I download the schedule to Excel?

Yes. The “Download CSV” button saves the table with unformatted numbers (a dot as the decimal separator, two decimals) that Excel opens as numbers. You can also copy the table and paste it into a sheet.

Lease or a car loan?

A lease usually gives a lower payment, but the car may not become yours automatically. A loan costs more per month but the car is yours at once. To compare, use the car lease calculator: the “Or a lease?” link passes your data to it.

How accurate is the calculation?

It is an estimate from standard formulas. Real loan terms, fees, insurance and the way the real rate is calculated are set by the bank, so check the result against the loan agreement.
