What the energy calculator does

The calculator solves mechanical energy problems: kinetic energy, the potential energy of a body and of a spring, the work of a force (with an angle), power, efficiency and the conservation of energy. Fill in the known quantities and units, and the calculator finds the rest.

Kinetic energy

The energy of a moving body is Eₖ = m · v² / 2. For m = 2 kg and v = 10 m/s it is 100 J. The energy grows with the square of the speed: doubling the speed quadruples it. 1 kJ = 1000 J, 1 kW·h = 3 600 000 J.

Potential energy

The energy of a body in a gravitational field is Eₚ = m · g · h. For m = 5 kg and h = 10 m with g = 9.8 m/s² it is 490 J. The energy of a compressed or stretched spring is Eₛ = k · x² / 2.

Work and power

The work of a force is A = F · s · cos α, where α is the angle between the force and the displacement. For F = 50 N, s = 10 m and α = 60° the work is 250 J, because cos 60° = 1/2. The power is N = A / t; if this work takes 5 s, N = 50 W. Also N = F · v. 1 hp ≈ 735.5 W.

Efficiency

Efficiency η = useful work / total work (or useful power / total power), written as a fraction or a percentage. If 80 J of the 100 J spent became useful, the efficiency is 0.8, that is 80 %. The efficiency of real machines is always less than 100 %.

Conservation of energy

Without friction the sum of the kinetic and potential energy is constant. A body falling from height h has at the bottom m · v² / 2 = m · g · h, hence v = √(2g · h), and the mass cancels. For h = 5 m and g = 9.8 m/s² the speed is √98 = 7√2 ≈ 9.9 m/s.

Units of energy and power

The basic unit of energy is the joule (1 J = 1 N · m). At home electricity is measured in kilowatt-hours: 1 kW·h = 3.6 MJ. Heat is often given in calories: 1 cal = 4.184 J and 1 kcal = 4184 J. Power is measured in watts (1 W = 1 J/s); the old unit horsepower is about 735.5 W. The calculator converts everything exactly, so you can enter, for example, 2 kW·h and get the result in joules or calories.

How work, energy and power are related

The work of a force changes the energy of a body: the work equals the increase of kinetic energy when only these forces act. Power N = A / t shows how fast work is done, and N = F · v is the power needed to move a body uniformly at speed v against a force F. Efficiency cannot exceed 100 % because part of the energy always goes into heat.

The value of g (9.8, 9.81 or 10 m/s²) is chosen in the settings. Roots are written exactly, for example 7√2.

FAQ

How do I find kinetic energy?

Eₖ = m · v² / 2. For 2 kg and 10 m/s it is 100 J.

How do I find potential energy?

Eₚ = m · g · h. For 5 kg and 10 m with g = 9.8 it is 490 J.

Why does energy depend on the square of the speed?

Because the work of the force accelerating a body is proportional to the distance, and the distance grows with the square of the speed. Doubling the speed gives four times the energy.

How do I find the work of a force?

A = F · s · cos α. If the force points along the displacement, α = 0 and A = F · s.

What is the work at an angle of 90°?

Zero: cos 90° = 0. A force perpendicular to the displacement does no work.

How do I find power?

N = A / t or N = F · v. The unit is the watt: 1 W = 1 J/s.

What is efficiency?

The ratio of useful work (or power) to total work (or power). For 80 J of 100 J it is 0.8 = 80 %.

Can efficiency exceed 100 %?

No, otherwise the conservation of energy would be violated.

How do I apply the conservation of energy?

Equate the initial and final energies: for a fall from height h, m · g · h = m · v² / 2, so v = √(2g · h).

How many joules are in a kilowatt-hour?

1 kW·h = 3 600 000 J.

Which value of g should I use?

9.8 m/s², more precisely 9.81, and 10 in school problems. The value is chosen in the settings.

Why is the answer sometimes written with a root?

Because, for example, the speed √(2g·h) for h = 5 m equals √98 = 7√2. A decimal value is shown beside it.

How many joules are in a calorie?

1 calorie = 4.184 J and 1 kilocalorie = 4184 J.

How many joules are in a kilowatt-hour?

1 kW·h = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 MJ.
