How the date of Easter is determined

Easter is the Sunday after the first spring full moon, but not before the equinox (21 March). The date is computed by the rules of the computus — tables that reconcile the solar and lunar cycles. Orthodox churches, including those in Ukraine, use the Julian computus: they count by the Julian calendar and convert the result to the modern one. Catholics and Protestants use the Gregorian computus. That is why the dates usually differ and coincide only in some years.

Date limits and movable feasts

Catholic Easter falls between 22 March and 25 April, and Orthodox Easter (in Gregorian terms in the 20th–21st centuries) between 4 April and 8 May. Other feasts depend on Easter: Palm Sunday a week before it, Good Friday two days before, Ascension on day 39 and Pentecost on day 49. Orthodox Great Lent begins with Clean Monday, 48 days before Easter, and Catholic Lent on Ash Wednesday, 46 days before. The “Easter in a year” tab shows all of this for the chosen year.

Examples: 2025, 2026 and 2027

In 2025 Easter was common to all on 20 April. In 2026 Catholics celebrate on 5 April and Orthodox on 12 April, a week later. In 2027 the gap is five weeks: Catholic Easter on 28 March and Orthodox on 2 May. In 2026 Orthodox Palm Sunday is 5 April, Ascension 21 May, Pentecost 31 May and Great Lent began on 23 February.

When the dates coincide

The dates coincide when the first spring full moon of the two calendars falls on the same Sunday. The nearest years of coincidence are 2025, 2028, 2031, 2034, 2037, 2038, 2041, 2045, 2048. The “Years list and coincidences” tab shows dates for several decades ahead and the nearest coincidences from the chosen year. The difference between the two Easters is always a multiple of seven days: in the 20th–21st centuries it is 0, 1, 4 or 5 weeks, and in the distant future other values appear: 6 weeks (from the 24th century) and 2 weeks (from the 28th).

Algorithms used by the calculator

For Orthodox Easter the Julian computus of Gauss is applied (giving a Julian date) and then converted to the Gregorian calendar through the Julian day number. For Catholic Easter the Meeus/Jones/Butcher Gregorian algorithm is used, which computes the golden number, the epact and century corrections. The results were checked against known dates for 2000–2030, including 2024 (31 March and 5 May) and 2025 (the common 20 April). The Gregorian calendar has been in force since 1583, hence the lower limit of the range.

When you need it

The date of Easter helps to plan holidays and trips, teaching material, days off at work and church or community events. The list of years shows how the date floats and when both Easters fall on the same day, which is handy for planning several years ahead.

Orthodox Easter is computed by the Julian computus and converted to the Gregorian calendar, Catholic Easter by the Gregorian one. The range of calculation is the years 1583–4099.

FAQ

When is Easter in 2026?

Orthodox on 12 April and Catholic on 5 April, a week apart.

Why does Easter fall on different days each year?

Because it is tied to the lunar calendar: it is the Sunday after the first spring full moon, so the date floats within several weeks.

Why do Orthodox and Catholic Easter differ?

The churches use different computus: the Orthodox by the Julian calendar and Catholics by the Gregorian, and the full moon is computed slightly differently.

When do both Easters coincide?

In 2025, 2028, 2031, 2034, 2037, 2038, 2041, 2045, 2048 and other years. The list is on the years tab.

What are the earliest and latest dates of Orthodox Easter?

In Gregorian terms today, from 4 April to 8 May (22 March to 25 April in the Julian calendar).

And of Catholic Easter?

From 22 March to 25 April.

When is Palm Sunday?

A week before Easter — exactly 7 days earlier.

When does Great Lent begin?

For the Orthodox, 48 days before Easter (Clean Monday); for Catholics, 46 days before (Ash Wednesday).

When are Ascension and Pentecost?

Ascension on day 39 after Easter (a Thursday), Pentecost on day 49 (a Sunday).

Does the Revised Julian calendar change Easter?

No, the Revised Julian calendar concerns only the fixed feasts. Orthodox churches in Ukraine still count Easter and the movable feasts by the Julian computus.

For which years does the calculator work?

For the years 1583–4099, from the beginning of the Gregorian calendar.

Why is the gap between the two Easters a multiple of seven days?

Both Easters are Sundays, and two Sundays are always a whole number of weeks apart. In the 20th–21st centuries that is 0, 1, 4 or 5 weeks (0, 7, 28 or 35 days); in the distant future 6 weeks (from the 24th century) and 2 weeks (from the 28th) also occur.
