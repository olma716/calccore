    Fuel and electricity cost calculator for cars

    What you can calculate

    Our online fuel cost calculator helps you estimate trip cost for gasoline, diesel, hybrid and electric cars. You can see the cost per 1 km, per 100 km, or for the entire route based on fuel price, consumption, electricity price and charging losses.

    The calculator can also price a round trip, split the cost between passengers, add tolls and parking and show how many kilometres your fuel budget will cover. Approximate prices for gasoline A-95 and A-92, diesel and LPG are provided and can be edited by hand.

    Units and currencies

    Enter the distance in kilometres, miles or nautical miles, fuel consumption in L/100 km, L/100 mi, km/L or mpg (US and UK), and the fuel price per litre or per gallon. The calculator converts everything to one system of units and shows the result in hryvnias.

    Visitors from abroad can choose a currency: USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, PLN, CZK, HUF, RON, CAD, AUD and TRY. Amounts you have entered and the approximate fuel prices are converted at the NBU rate, and all results are shown in the selected currency.

    Comparing two cars

    With this tool you can easily compare two cars — for example diesel vs hybrid, or EV vs gasoline. It shows which car is cheaper to run and how much money you can save per year.

    If you’re considering buying a car from Germany or Europe, this calculator helps you understand real running costs across powertrains, especially when comparing EVs, hybrids and classic ICE vehicles.

    What it is for

    Use it for trip planning, budget estimation, choosing a car before purchase, and analyzing yearly fuel or electricity expenses. This free calculator helps you optimize your budget, reduce running costs and choose the most cost-effective vehicle for daily commuting or long trips.

    FAQ about car running costs

    How much does 100 km cost on gasoline?

    It depends on fuel consumption and price per liter. For example, 8 L/100 km at 55 UAH/L costs 440 UAH per 100 km.

    How much does 1 km cost in an electric car?

    If an EV uses 18 kWh per 100 km and electricity costs 6 UAH per kWh, then 1 km costs about 1.08 UAH (before losses).

    What’s cheaper — an EV or a gasoline car?

    In most cases an EV is cheaper to run, especially with frequent driving and home charging. This calculator helps compare EV, hybrid and ICE precisely.

    How do I calculate trip expenses?

    Enter trip distance, fuel or electricity consumption and price. The calculator shows total cost, cost per 1 km and per 100 km.

    Can I compare two cars?

    Yes. Click “Add Car 2” and compare any two cars — diesel vs hybrid or EV vs gasoline — to see which is cheaper to operate.

    How do I calculate a round trip?

    Turn on the “Round trip” switch: the calculator doubles the route distance and calculates fuel and cost for the whole journey.

    How much does a trip cost per person?

    Enter the number of people in the car: the calculator splits the trip cost equally and shows the amount per passenger.

    How far will my fuel budget take me?

    Enter an amount in the “Fuel budget” field: the calculator shows how many kilometres and litres you can cover with that money at your consumption and fuel price.

    Can I enter the distance in miles?

    Yes. Choose “miles” or “nmi” (nautical miles) next to the distance field: the calculator converts them to kilometres (1 mile ≈ 1.609 km, 1 nautical mile = 1.852 km) and shows the cost per 1 km and per 1 mile. Fuel consumption can be entered in L/100 km, L/100 mi, km/L or mpg.

    Can I enter the fuel price per gallon?

    Yes. Next to the price field choose “UAH / US gal” or “UAH / UK gal”: the calculator converts the price to litres (1 US gallon ≈ 3.785 L, 1 UK gallon ≈ 4.546 L) and shows the result in hryvnias.

    How do I include tolls and parking?

    Open the “Costs and budget” block and enter the tolls (one way — doubled for a round trip) and parking for the whole trip: these amounts are added to the fuel cost in the total result.

    Can I calculate in another currency?

    Yes. Choose a currency at the top of the calculator: USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, PLN, CZK, HUF, RON, CAD, AUD or TRY. Amounts you've entered and the approximate fuel prices are converted at the NBU rate, and all results are shown in the selected currency.
