How net pay is calculated

From the gross salary (before tax) a personal income tax of 18% and a military levy of 5% are withheld, 23% in total. What remains is the net pay. For a salary of 30,000 UAH: income tax 5,400 UAH, military levy 1,500 UAH, net pay 23,100 UAH. For 100,000 UAH the net pay is 77,000 UAH.

In the reverse mode the calculator finds the gross salary that gives the desired net pay after tax: gross = net ÷ (1 − 0.23). To receive 30,000 UAH net you need a gross of 38,961.04 UAH.

ESV and the cost of an employee to the employer

The 22% ESV (single social contribution) is paid by the employer on top and does not reduce the net pay. For a salary of 30,000 UAH it is 6,600 UAH, so the employee costs the employer 36,600 UAH and the tax burden (income tax, military levy and ESV as a share of the employer cost) is 36.9%.

ESV cannot be charged on less than the minimum wage (for full-time work). From 1 January 2026 it is 8,647 UAH, and the minimum ESV is 1,902.34 UAH. So for a salary of 5,000 UAH the ESV is 1,902.34 UAH, not 1,100 UAH. By default the calculator does not apply an upper ESV base limit: enter it yourself if it applies.

Bonuses, deductions and currency

A bonus is taxed like a salary. A one-time bonus of 25,000 UAH in December on top of a salary of 50,000 UAH raises the net pay for the year to 481,250 UAH and the December net pay to 57,750 UAH. Deductions (alimony, union dues) are subtracted after tax: with a salary of 30,000 UAH and 3,000 UAH of deductions the net pay is 20,100 UAH.

For a salary in a foreign currency the taxes are calculated the same way, and the exchange rate is needed only to apply the minimum ESV base in hryvnias. The rates and the minimum wage in the “Law parameters” block can be changed.

The calculation is an estimate. Tax rates, the minimum wage and the ESV limits are set by law and may change; the real pay depends on the contract, the worked time and the employer's rules.

FAQ

How do I work out the net pay from the gross salary?

Subtract the 18% income tax and the 5% military levy (23% in total). For a salary of 30,000 UAH: 5,400 UAH of income tax, 1,500 UAH of levy, net pay 23,100 UAH.

Which taxes are withheld from a salary?

Personal income tax of 18% and a military levy of 5%. For 30,000 UAH that is 5,400 and 1,500 UAH. The rates are set by law and may change, so you can change them in the calculator.

What is ESV and who pays it?

The single social contribution of 22% is paid by the employer on top of the accrued salary. It does not reduce the net pay. For 30,000 UAH it is 6,600 UAH and the full cost of the employee is 36,600 UAH.

What is the minimum ESV base?

ESV is charged on at least the minimum wage (for full-time work). From 1 January 2026 it is 8,647 UAH, so the minimum ESV is 1,902.34 UAH. For a salary of 5,000 UAH the calculator takes 1,902.34 UAH, not 1,100 UAH. The value can be changed.

Is there an upper limit of the ESV base?

The upper limit of the ESV base depends on the law in force, so the calculator does not apply it by default. If it applies in your case, enter it in the “Law parameters” block.

How do I work out the gross salary from a desired net pay?

Choose the “Net → gross” mode. Gross = net ÷ (1 − 0.23). To receive 30,000 UAH you need a gross of 38,961.04 UAH.

How do I include a bonus or a 13th salary?

Enter a monthly bonus (as a percentage or an amount) or a one-time one in the right month. It is taxed like a salary. A one-time 25,000 UAH in December on top of 50,000 UAH gives 57,750 UAH net in December.

What is the tax burden?

The share of income tax, military levy and ESV in the employer's total cost. For a salary of 30,000 UAH it is 36.9%: out of 36,600 UAH of cost the employee gets 23,100 UAH.

How do I calculate a salary in a foreign currency?

Choose USD or EUR. The taxes are calculated the same way, and the rate (UAH per 1 unit) is needed only for the minimum ESV base. Without a rate the minimum base is not applied, and the calculator warns you.

Does the calculator include the tax social benefit, holiday pay and sick pay?

No. The tax social benefit applies only to a small income (about 4,660 UAH per month in 2026 according to open sources), so it does not apply to ordinary salaries. Holiday pay, sick pay and worked time depend on the contract and the employer's rules: check with the payroll department.
