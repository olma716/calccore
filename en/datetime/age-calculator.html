How exact age is calculated

The calculator finds the full difference between the date of birth and the chosen date in years, months and days. First it finds the last anniversary, i.e. how many whole years you have turned, then adds whole months, and the rest is days. For example, for someone born on June 15, 1990, on March 10, 2025 the age is 34 years 8 months 23 days. In total that is 12,687 days, or 1,812 weeks and 3 days, or 416 whole months.

If you also give a time of birth (for example 14:30), hours, minutes and seconds appear: for someone born on June 15, 1990 at 14:30, on March 10, 2025 at 00:00 that is 304,473 hours, 18,268,410 minutes and 1,096,104,600 seconds. Without a time of birth the calculator does not show them because they cannot be counted exactly. For “Today” they are counted at the current moment.

Months of different lengths

Months have different numbers of days, so the calculator adds whole months to the date of birth and, if the target month has no such day, uses its last day: January 31 plus 1 month is February 28. Thanks to that, from January 31, 2023 to March 1, 2023 you get 1 month 1 day, and from March 31, 2024 to May 1, 2024 also 1 month 1 day, with no negative or lost days. The difference in days is calculated in UTC, so a daylight saving time change shifts nothing.

Born on 29 February

Non-leap years have no 29 February, and different customs move the birthday to 28 February or to 1 March. In documents the date stays 29 February, and I found no direct rule in Ukrainian law, so the calculator lets you choose. For someone born on 29.02.2000, on March 10, 2025 the age is 25 years 10 days under the 28 February rule or 25 years 9 days under the 1 March rule.

The next birthday

The calculator shows the date of the next birthday, its weekday, how old you will turn and how many days are left, plus a table of the ten nearest birthdays. For someone born on June 15, 1990, on March 10, 2025 the next birthday is June 15, 2025, a Sunday, turning 35, with 97 days left. The weekday of the birth itself is a Friday.

The age gap between two people

On the “Age gap” tab enter two dates of birth — the calculator shows who is older and by how much in years, months, days and weeks. For June 15, 1990 and February 28, 1994 the gap is 3 years 8 months 13 days, i.e. 1,354 days. It also shows each person's age on the chosen date and the ratio of the ages. If they were born on the same day, the calculator says so.

Round dates of your life

The “Round dates” tab finds when you reach a nice number of days, weeks, months, hours or seconds. For someone born on June 15, 1990, 10,000 days were reached on October 31, 2017 (a Tuesday), and 15,000 days will be reached on July 10, 2031 (a Thursday). If you give a time of birth, hours and seconds also appear in the table: for someone born on 06/15/1990 at 14:30 a billion seconds were reached on February 21, 2022 at 16:16:40. Without a time of birth hours and seconds are not shown. You can also enter your own amount, for example 12,345 days.

Limits

The calculation uses the Gregorian calendar; for dates before 1582, when it was introduced, the result is nominal, and there is a separate converter for Julian dates. Hours, minutes and seconds are shown only when a time of birth is given and use the local clock without a daylight saving adjustment. For official cases (majority, retirement, insurance) rely on documents and laws, not on an online calculator.

FAQ

How do I calculate age in years, months and days?

Find the last anniversary, add whole months and count the rest in days. The calculator does it automatically: for June 15, 1990 on March 10, 2025 it is 34 years 8 months 23 days.

How do I find how many days I have lived?

Enter the date of birth: the calculator shows the total days lived. For June 15, 1990 on March 10, 2025 it is 12,687 days.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Yes, change the “Calculate on” field. The calculator shows the age you will be on that day.

When is my next birthday?

The calculator shows its date, weekday, how old you will turn and how many days are left, plus a table of the next ten.

When do I celebrate if I was born on 29 February?

In non-leap years people usually choose 28 February or 1 March. In documents the date stays 29 February. The calculator lets you choose either.

What is an age gap?

The span between two dates of birth. For June 15, 1990 and February 28, 1994 it is 3 years 8 months 13 days, or 1,354 days.

When will I be 10,000 days old?

Add 10,000 days to your date of birth: for June 15, 1990 that is October 31, 2017. The “Round dates” tab does it automatically.

When is a billion seconds of life?

After 11,574 days 1 hour 46 minutes 40 seconds from the moment of birth, at about 31 years 8 months. For June 15, 1990 at 14:30 that is February 21, 2022 at 16:16:40. Without a time of birth seconds are not shown.

Why do months have different days and how is that handled?

The calculator adds whole months with an end-of-month limit (January 31 plus 1 month is February 28), so days are never negative.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes, all differences use the real calendar, including 29 February.

Are the hours and seconds exact?

Yes, if you give a time of birth (hh:mm). Without it hours, minutes and seconds are not shown because they cannot be counted exactly.

Is the calculator suitable for dates before 1582?

The result is nominal because the calculation uses the Gregorian calendar. For old Julian dates there is a separate converter.
