What the CMU 2010 transliteration is

Transliteration writes the words of one language with the letters of another. For Ukrainian names and place names in documents the system approved by Resolution No. 55 of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine of 27 January 2010 is used. Foreign passports, ID cards and other documents, as well as geographic names, are written by it. The calculator applies exactly these rules: one Cyrillic letter corresponds to one Latin letter or combination, and the rest depends on the position in the word.

Special letters: Є, Ї, Й, Ю, Я

These five letters are written differently depending on their place in a word. At the start of a word: Є → Ye, Ї → Yi, Й → Y, Ю → Yu, Я → Ya. Elsewhere: Є → ie, Ї → i, Й → i, Ю → iu, Я → ia. That is why Єнакієве is Yenakiieve, Юрій is Yurii, Олексій is Oleksii and Вінниця is Vinnytsia. All other letters have a constant correspondence: Г → h, Ґ → g, Х → kh, Ц → ts, Ч → ch, Ш → sh, Щ → shch, Ж → zh.

The “зг” combination, the soft sign and the apostrophe

To avoid reading “зг” as “zh”, it is written as “zgh”: Згорани is Zghorany. The soft sign and the apostrophe are not transliterated: Львів is Lviv, Мар'яна is Mariana. Double consonants are kept: Ілля is Illia, Запоріжжя is Zaporizhzhia.

How to write a full name for documents

The “Full name for documents” tab takes the surname, given name and optionally the patronymic in separate fields and gives the capitals as in a foreign passport: SHEVCHENKO TARAS. For a double surname with a hyphen you can choose whether the letter after the hyphen counts as a word start: by default it does, so Бойко-Юрченко gives Boiko-Yurchenko and not Boiko-Iurchenko. If your document already has a Latin spelling, keep it — the name should be the same in all documents.

Common mistakes and limits

The most frequent confusions are Г (h, not g), Х (kh, not h) and the final Й (Андрій is Andrii, not Andriy). Old passports and visas may have used another system, so their spelling may differ from the calculator's result. The system conveys letters, not pronunciation: it is meant for documents and databases, not for reading aloud.

Where it is needed

Transliteration is needed when applying for a foreign passport or a visa, ordering a bank card, booking tickets, registering on foreign sites, signing international contracts and creating Latin URLs or file names. The “Text” tab handles whole sentences: Latin letters and digits stay unchanged, and Cyrillic ones are replaced by the table. The result can be copied with the button below it.

The result follows Resolution No. 55 of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine of 27 Jan 2010. For official documents match the spelling with the documents you already have (passport, bank card).

FAQ

How do I transliterate a Ukrainian name into Latin?

By the CMU 2010 system: enter the name on the “Full name for documents” tab. For example Юрій becomes Yurii, Олександр becomes Oleksandr.

What is the difference between Ye and ie for the letter Є?

At the start of a word Є is Ye (Євген — Yevhen), inside or at the end of a word it is ie (Дмитрієв — Dmytriiev).

Why is Юрій written Yurii and not Yuriy?

The letter Й inside and at the end of a word is written as i, so Юрій is Yurii. The “y” variant is old and belongs to other systems.

How is the letter Г written?

As h (Галина — Halyna). The letter Ґ is g (Ґудзик — Gudzyk).

What about the soft sign and the apostrophe?

They are dropped: Львів is Lviv, Мар'яна is Mariana.

Why does “зг” become “zgh”?

To avoid reading the combination as “zh”. Згорани is Zghorany. This rule is part of the official table.

Does the case matter?

Yes. A capital letter at the start of a word stays capital, and words in all capitals give capitals: ЮРІЙ → YURII.

How do I transliterate a hyphenated surname?

Each part as a separate word: Бойко-Яворський is Boiko-Yavorskyi. You can choose another treatment of the letter after the hyphen in the tab setting.

What happens to Latin letters and digits in the text?

They stay unchanged: Київ 2024 → Kyiv 2024. Only Ukrainian Cyrillic letters are transliterated.

Can I transliterate Russian letters?

The calculator is meant for the Ukrainian alphabet. Letters absent from the Ukrainian alphabet stay unchanged.

Does the result match my foreign passport?

For new documents, yes, by the CMU rules. In older passports and foreign bank cards the spelling may differ; in that case keep the spelling already used.

Is my text stored on a server?

No, the conversion happens in your browser and the text is not sent anywhere.
