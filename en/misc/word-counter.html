Why you need a word counter

Word and character counting is useful in many situations: writing essays with length requirements, staying within social media character limits, optimizing SEO metadata, or simply gauging text length before publishing.

What the counter shows

  • Words: total word count in the text.
  • Characters with spaces: full text length, including spaces between words.
  • Characters without spaces: useful for platforms that only count visible characters.
  • Sentences: sentence count (determined by punctuation marks .!?)
  • Paragraphs: number of text blocks separated by blank lines.
  • Read time: estimated time needed to read the text (based on average reading speed).

Typical character limits for various platforms

  • Twitter/X: 280 characters per post.
  • SEO title: recommended 50-60 characters to avoid truncation in search results.
  • Meta description: recommended 150-160 characters.
  • Instagram caption: up to 2200 characters, though the visible portion is shorter before expansion.

The count updates in real time as you type or paste text — no need to click any additional buttons.

FAQ

How are words counted?

Words are defined as sequences of characters separated by spaces or punctuation marks.

Are spaces counted in the character count?

Two values are shown: character count with and without spaces — choose based on the requirements of your specific platform or task.

Why is character counting important for SEO?

Search engines truncate overly long titles and meta descriptions in results, so it's important to stay within recommended character limits (usually 50-60 for titles, 150-160 for descriptions).
