What the construction cost calculator counts

The calculator builds the budget by the formula quantity × your price for every stage, then adds a contingency, VAT and extra costs. There are no built-in prices: you enter the prices from your contractors’ offers, so the result follows your terms rather than an averaged database. The modes are House, Flat renovation and Foundation (strip and slab).

The contingency is counted on the sum of the stages, VAT on the stages together with the contingency, and the extra costs (design, permits, utility connections, landscaping) are added as a separate line with all taxes already included. All prices can be entered in hryvnia, dollars or euros.

House mode: footprint and floors

The foundation and the roof depend on the footprint, while walls, floors, windows, utilities and finishing depend on the total area of all floors. That is why a two-storey house costs less per m² than a one-storey one of the same area. An example with illustrative prices (foundation $90, walls $130, roof $70 per m²): 200 m² on one floor cost $58,000, that is $290 per m², while the same 200 m² on two floors with a 100 m² footprint cost $42,000, that is $210 per m².

The readiness sets the stages: shell — foundation, walls and floors, roof; rough finish adds windows, doors, facade and utilities; turnkey adds interior finishing as well. The result shows what is included in the total and what is not.

If you enter the roof pitch, the roof area is counted as the footprint divided by the cosine of the angle, and the roof price should be entered per m² of roof. For a 100 m² footprint and a 35° pitch the roof area is about 122.1 m²: at $70 per m² that is $8,545, while by the footprint it would be $7,000. Without the pitch the roof is counted by the footprint.

Flat renovation mode

All stages are counted by the area of the flat. A cosmetic renovation covers the finishing of walls and ceilings, the floor, doors and small work; a capital one adds demolition, rough work (screed, plaster), electrics, plumbing and heating. An example with illustrative prices: a capital renovation of 60 m² at a combined price of $145/m² gives $8,700, or $9,570 with a 10% contingency, that is $159.5 per m². A cosmetic renovation of the same flat at the same prices costs $4,620.

Foundation mode

The concrete volume of a strip foundation equals the length of the strip (the perimeter 2 × (length + width) plus the inner walls) times the width and the height; for a slab it is length × width × thickness. Rebar is counted as kg per m³ of concrete and formwork by the area of the side surfaces. An example: a 10 × 8 m building with 6 m of inner walls and a 0.4 × 0.8 m strip. The strip length is 42 m and the concrete volume is 13.44 m³. At illustrative prices the total with a 10% contingency is $4,265, that is $317.3 per m³ of concrete.

Fence mode

The number of posts equals the fence length divided by the spacing, rounded up, plus one; the infill area (sections, sheets, pickets) is length × height. The fence foundation and installation are counted by the length, gates and wickets by the number. An example with illustrative prices: 40 m of fence 2 m high with a post spacing of 2.5 m is 17 posts and 80 m² of infill, a base of $4,145, or $4,560 with a 10% contingency, that is $114 per metre.

Garage and outbuildings mode

The mode has three types: a garage (gates), an outbuilding (shed, workshop; doors) and a bathhouse or summer kitchen (doors, interior lining and insulation, equipment). The foundation, floor, roof and electrics are counted by the footprint, walls and lining by the wall area 2 × (length + width) × height without deducting openings, gates, doors and equipment by the number. An example with illustrative prices: a 6 × 4 m garage with 2.5 m walls has a footprint of 24 m² and a wall area of 50 m², a base of $5,820, or $6,402 with a 10% contingency, that is $266.8 per m² of footprint.

Price guides for 2026

There is no single price: it depends on the region, materials and the contractor. According to a designer’s calculation quoted by Novyny.LIVE, in 2026 the shell of a one-storey house (foundation, walls, roof) costs about $395 per m², without windows, heating and finishing. This is one specialist’s opinion, not a standard.

Ukraine’s Ministry of Development published the cost indicators of housing construction for the second quarter of 2026: 26,623 UAH per m² including VAT on average (Ligazakon). It is an indicator for publicly funded housing, not the market price of a private house.

The calculator gives an approximate budget from your own prices, not a bill of quantities. The exact cost is set by the design and the contractors’ offers.

FAQ

What does the construction cost calculator count?

The budget of a house, a flat renovation or a foundation: the quantity of every stage is multiplied by your price, then the contingency, VAT and extra costs are added. There are no built-in prices.

Why is a two-storey house cheaper per m²?

The foundation and the roof depend on the footprint, not on the number of floors. At illustrative prices 200 m² on one floor cost $58,000 ($290 per m²), and on two floors $42,000 ($210 per m²).

What does “Shell” include?

The foundation, walls and floors, and the roof. Windows, doors, utilities and finishing are not included at this level.

How does “Rough finish” differ from “Turnkey”?

“Rough finish” adds windows, doors, facade and utilities to the shell. “Turnkey” also adds interior finishing.

How does a cosmetic renovation differ from a capital one?

A cosmetic one is the finishing of walls and ceilings, the floor, doors and small work. A capital one adds demolition, rough work, electrics, plumbing and heating. At illustrative prices for 60 m² it is $4,620 against $9,570.

How do I calculate the concrete volume of a strip foundation?

Multiply the length of the strip (the perimeter plus the inner walls) by the width and the height. For a 10 × 8 m building with 6 m of inner walls and a 0.4 × 0.8 m strip it is 42 m × 0.4 × 0.8 = 13.44 m³.

How do I calculate the concrete volume of a slab foundation?

Multiply the length by the width and the thickness of the slab. For 10 × 8 m and a thickness of 0.3 m it is 24 m³.

How much rebar should I allow?

The calculator does not assume a norm: enter the kg of rebar per m³ of concrete from your design. For 13.44 m³ of concrete and 80 kg/m³ (an illustrative figure) it is 1,075 kg.

How do I account for the roof pitch?

Enter the roof pitch: the roof area equals the footprint divided by the cosine of the angle. For a 100 m² footprint and 35° it is about 122.1 m²; at $70 per m² of roof that is $8,545 instead of $7,000 by the footprint. The overhang and complex shapes are not counted.

How do I calculate the number of fence posts?

Divide the length by the post spacing, round up and add one. For 40 m and a spacing of 2.5 m it is 16 + 1 = 17 posts.

What does the fence calculation include?

The fence foundation, posts, infill, gates, a wicket and installation. Only the infill is required, the rest can be left out. The infill area is length × height: for 40 m and 2 m it is 80 m².

How do I calculate the cost of a garage?

Multiply the footprint by the prices of the foundation, floor, roof and electrics, the wall area by the price of walls, and add the gates. For a 6 × 4 m garage with 2.5 m walls at illustrative prices it is $5,820, or $6,402 with a 10% contingency.

How do the building types differ in the “Garage and outbuildings” mode?

A garage has gates, a shed or workshop has doors, a bathhouse or summer kitchen has doors, interior lining with insulation and equipment (a stove or boiler). The foundation, floor, walls, roof and electrics are in every type.

What contingency should I set aside?

Sources name anything from 5–10% to 10–20% depending on the complexity and the project stage; for a typical private house about 10% is often used.

How do I include VAT?

If the contractor’s prices exclude VAT, enter 20%: it is applied to the stages together with the contingency. Enter the extra costs with all taxes already included.

How much does building 1 m² cost in 2026?

There is no single standard. By one designer’s estimate, the shell of a one-storey house costs about $395 per m² without windows, heating and finishing. For publicly funded housing the Ministry of Development gives 26,623 UAH per m² including VAT on average for the second quarter of 2026.

Is the calculator suitable for a bill of quantities?

It is an approximate budget from your own prices. An exact estimate needs a design, the quantities of work and the contractors’ commercial offers.
