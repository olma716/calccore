What the competitive score calculator does

The “Calculate the score” tab computes the competitive score by the NMT 2026/2025 formula (and 2024 and 2023) with all the coefficients and shows every step. “Which fourth subject is better” compares several options of the elective subject with your scores and weights. “Which score is needed” solves the reverse task: it finds the score in a subject needed for a target competitive score.

The 2026 competitive score formula

For NMT 2026 and 2025 results: CS = (K1·S1 + K2·S2 + K3·S3 + K4·S4 + CW·CC) / (K1 + K2 + K3 + (K4max + K4)/2 + CW) + EP. Here S are the scores on the 100–200 scale, K the weights of the specialty, K4max the largest weight among the elective subjects, CW and CC the weight and score of the creative contest and EP the extra points. For NMT 2024 the denominator is simply K1 + K2 + K3 + K4, and for NMT 2023 three subjects are counted. In the Ukrainian rules these quantities are written РК, ГК, ОУ, КТ, ТК and КБ: here they are CS (КБ), S (П), K (К), CW (КТ), CC (ТК), EP (ОУ), RC (РК) and SC (ГК).

A worked example

Subject scores 170, 185, 160 and 175, weights K1 = 0.3, K2 = 0.4, K3 = 0.2, K4 = 0.3 and K4max = 0.4. The numerator: 51 + 74 + 32 + 52.5 = 209.5. The denominator: 0.3 + 0.4 + 0.2 + (0.4 + 0.3)/2 = 1.25. CS = 209.5 / 1.25 = 167.6. With a regional coefficient of 1.07 it becomes 167.6 · 1.07 = 179.332, and with EP = 5 it is (167.6 + 5) · 1.07 = 184.682. By the NMT 2024 formula the same data would give 174.583 because the fourth subject then counts with its full weight.

RC, SC, EP and the 200 cap

After the weighted average is computed and EP added, the result is multiplied by the regional coefficient (RC), which depends on the location of the institution: by open 2026 sources it is 1.07 for the Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv, Odesa, Sumy and Kharkiv regions, 1.04 for Dnipropetrovsk, Poltava and Chernihiv, and 1.00 for the rest. The sector coefficient (SC) is 1.02 if applications with priorities 1 and 2 are submitted to state-supported specialties, otherwise 1. EP is up to 10 points for preparatory courses and 15 for the zero course (for admission to a specialty at that institution). The competitive score is rounded to thousandths and cannot exceed 200.

Minimum scores and choosing the fourth subject

Under the 2026 rules the competitive score for a budget place must be at least 130 (150 for some specialties), and the threshold of 100 must be passed in every NMT subject. The fourth subject can be any, but each specialty has a profile one with a higher weight. As a rule the subject with the larger product of weight and score pays more, but K4 also enters the denominator, so only a comparison gives the exact answer. Use the “Which fourth subject is better” tab to compare: in the example foreign language gives 174.615, physics 170 and biology 162.5.

Where to get the weights

The weights K1–K4 differ for each specialty and institution: they are published in the admission rules of the institution and in admission information systems. In the calculator you enter them by hand, so use the values for your competitive offer. The scores S can be obtained from test points with the NMT score converter.

The formulas and coefficients follow the 2026 admission rules from open sources; the regional coefficients are preliminary. Take the weights K from the admission rules of your institution.

FAQ

How is the competitive score calculated?

By CS = (K1·S1 + K2·S2 + K3·S3 + K4·S4 + CW·CC) / (K1 + K2 + K3 + (K4max + K4)/2 + CW) + EP, then multiplied by RC and SC and capped at 200.

What are weights?

The shares with which subjects enter the competitive score; they are set by the specialty and the institution. Profile subjects have a higher weight.

What is K4max?

The largest weight among the elective subjects of this specialty. For NMT 2026 and 2025 the denominator holds the average of it and the weight of your fourth subject.

What is RC?

The regional coefficient of the institution: 1.07 or 1.04 for certain regions and 1.00 for the rest. It multiplies the competitive score.

What is SC?

A sector coefficient of 1.02 if applications with priorities 1 and 2 are submitted to state-supported specialties; otherwise 1.

What is EP?

Extra points for the institution's preparatory courses (up to 10) or the zero course (15). They are added to the weighted average before multiplying by RC and SC.

Can the competitive score exceed 200?

No: if the product with the coefficients is higher, 200 is taken.

What is the minimum competitive score for a budget place?

130 points, and 150 for some specialties. The threshold of 100 must also be passed in every NMT subject.

How do I choose the fourth subject?

Compare the score for several options: the “Which fourth subject is better” tab computes them with your scores and weights.

Which scores do I enter, test or rating?

Rating scores on the 100–200 scale. Convert test points with the NMT score converter.

Do the formulas differ for NMT 2025, 2024 and 2023?

For 2026 and 2025 the formula is the same, for 2024 the denominator holds K4 without averaging, and for 2023 three subjects are counted.

Can I rely on these coefficients for admission?

The calculator follows the rules from open sources; the regional coefficients are preliminary and the final values are approved by the Ministry of Education and Science. Check them against the official rules before applying.
