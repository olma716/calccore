What the converter does

The converter converts oven temperature between degrees Celsius, Fahrenheit and gas mark. American recipes give the temperature in Fahrenheit, most others in Celsius, and British cookbooks often in gas marks from ¼ to 9. The calculator shows all three values at once, together with the equivalent for a fan oven, the name of the mode (moderate, hot and so on) and a handy value for the dial.

Formulas

From Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, so 180 °C = 356 °F and 200 °C = 392 °F. From Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9, so 350 °F ≈ 177 °C and 400 °F ≈ 204 °C. Recipes round to handy values: 350 °F is treated as 175 °C, 400 °F as 200 °C and 425 °F as 220 °C. The calculator shows the exact value and, separately, a handy one for the dial (steps of 5 °C or 25 °F).

Gas mark

Gas mark is a traditional British scale for gas ovens. In the table for a conventional oven gas mark ¼ is 110 °C (225 °F), mark 1 is 140 °C, mark 3 is 170 °C (325 °F), mark 4 is 180 °C (350 °F), mark 6 is 200 °C (400 °F), mark 7 is 220 °C and mark 9 is 240 °C (475 °F). For temperatures between rows the calculator interpolates: 175 °C is about gas mark 3.5 (between 3 and 4). For mark 3 sources differ: Currys gives 170 °C while Which? gives 160 °C, so the calculator uses 170 °C.

Fan oven

In fan (convection) ovens hot air circulates and food cooks faster, so the temperature is usually reduced by about 20 °C (≈ 25 °F). Choose in the calculator which oven the recipe temperature is for: for a conventional oven it shows the fan value, and for a fan oven it raises the value for a conventional one. You can change the reduction in the settings if your oven's manual says otherwise. For fan-assisted gas ovens some sources advise reducing the gas mark by 1.

Conversion tables

The “Table” tab shows gas marks ¼–9 with degrees Celsius and Fahrenheit, the fan value and the mode name. There are also tables of °C → °F (100 to 260 °C in steps of 10) and °F → °C (225 to 500 °F in steps of 25) with the gas mark for each row. The modes are very cool (marks ¼–½), cool (1–2), warm (3), moderate (4), moderately hot (5–6), hot (7–8) and very hot (9).

Temperatures in a recipe text

The “Temperatures in text” tab finds entries such as 350°F, 180 °C, 350 degrees F, 180 degrees, 350° or gas mark 4 in the pasted text and replaces them in the chosen scale. A range 350–375°F becomes 175–190 °C. The rest of the text stays unchanged. If no scale is given, the one set in the settings is used. You can say which oven the recipe is for and which you have, and the temperatures will shift by the adjustment.

Why a recipe temperature is not always exact

The real temperature depends on the oven: thermostats can be off by tens of degrees and different zones of an oven run differently. So for exact baking an oven thermometer helps, and it is worth going by how the dish looks rather than only by temperature and time. The values in the calculator are approximate.

Limits

The calculator accepts temperatures up to 500 °C (932 °F) and gas marks from ¼ to 9. Temperatures below 100 °C or above 300 °C are flagged as unusual for an oven. In the text, numbers without a scale mark that fall outside possible oven temperatures are ignored.

The gas mark follows the table for a conventional oven, with values between rows interpolated. For a fan oven the temperature is usually reduced by about 20 °C (≈ 25 °F), but check your oven's manual.

FAQ

How many degrees Fahrenheit is 180 degrees Celsius?

180 °C = 356 °F. Recipes usually round it to 350 °F.

What is 350 °F in Celsius?

350 °F ≈ 177 °C, usually written as 175 °C in recipes (gas mark 4 is 180 °C).

What is a gas mark?

It is a British scale of gas oven temperatures from ¼ to 9. Mark 4 is 180 °C (350 °F) and mark 6 is 200 °C (400 °F).

What temperature is gas mark 4?

180 °C or 350 °F for a conventional oven.

What temperature is gas mark 3?

170 °C (325 °F) in the Currys table; other tables, such as Which?, give 160 °C. The calculator uses 170 °C.

By how much should I lower the temperature for a fan oven?

Usually by about 20 °C (≈ 25 °F). This is a general rule and you can change the amount in the settings.

What is convection in an oven?

It is a mode with a fan that circulates hot air. Food heats faster and more evenly, so the temperature is lowered.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius by hand?

Subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9: (350 − 32) × 5/9 ≈ 177 °C.

Why do American recipes use Fahrenheit?

The Fahrenheit scale is still used in everyday life in the US, including cooking.

Can I convert all temperatures in a recipe at once?

Yes, on the “Temperatures in text” tab paste the text and choose the scale: every temperature found is replaced and the rest of the text stays.

Which temperature counts as a moderate oven?

A moderate oven is gas mark 4, that is 180 °C (350 °F). Marks 5–6 are moderately hot, 7–8 hot and 9 very hot.

Is the oven temperature exact?

No, thermostats often differ from the real temperature by several tens of degrees. For accuracy use an oven thermometer.
