How the state old-age pension is calculated

The old-age pension is calculated by the formula P = Wp × Ke. Wp is the average wage for pensions (17,482.87 UAH for new pensions of 2026, the 2023–2025 average) multiplied by your salary coefficient Kz, that is by the ratio of your salary to the national average. Ke is the experience coefficient: 1% for each year of insurance experience.

For a salary of 30,000 UAH (with a national average of 32,243 UAH) and 35 years of experience the pension comes to about 5,693 UAH, which is 19% of the salary. For 20 years of experience it is 3,253 UAH. With the required experience the pension cannot be below the minimum (2,595 UAH) or above the maximum of 25,950 UAH (10 subsistence minimums).

Experience and retirement age

In 2026 retiring at 60 requires 33 years of insurance experience (the requirement grows by a year every year up to 35 in 2028), at 63 — 23 years (up to 25), at 65 — 15 years. The calculator shows whether your experience is enough for each of these ages in the year you reach them and what you are short of.

This is a simplified model: it assumes you work without breaks and your salary stays the same relative to the national average. The real coefficient Kz is calculated over the whole career, and the rules may change.

Your own savings and retirement income

The state pension is usually much lower than the salary, so own savings are needed. If you save 3,000 UAH a month with 200,000 UAH at the start, a 5% yearly indexation of contributions and a 12% return, in 30 years you accumulate 23,010,146 UAH, but with 7% inflation that is only 3,022,777 UAH in today’s money — about 20,260 UAH a month over 20 years of retirement.

An earlier start helps a lot: 3,000 UAH a month at 12% over 35 years gives 19,485,807 UAH, and over 25 years 5,692,905 UAH (without inflation).

The law parameters are as of 2026 from secondary sources (average wage for pensions 17,482.87 UAH, minimum 2,595 UAH, maximum 25,950 UAH); you can change them in the fields. The calculation is approximate.

FAQ

How do I calculate a pension in Ukraine?

Choose “State pension”, enter your age, retirement age (60, 63 or 65), experience and salary. The calculator uses P = Wp × Ke. For a salary of 30,000 UAH and 35 years of experience it is about 5,693 UAH per month (19% of the salary).

What is the formula of the old-age pension?

P = Wp × Ke, where Wp = the average wage for pensions × Kz, Kz is the ratio of your salary to the national average, and Ke = years of experience × 1%. For new pensions of 2026 the average wage is 17,482.87 UAH (2023–2025).

How much experience is needed for a pension?

In 2026: 33 years to retire at 60, 23 years at 63, 15 years at 65. The requirement for 60 grows by a year every year up to 35 in 2028, for 63 up to 25.

What are the minimum and the maximum pension?

The minimum old-age pension is 2,595 UAH (equal to the subsistence minimum for those who lost working capacity), the maximum is 25,950 UAH (10 subsistence minimums). The minimum applies if the required experience is enough.

Why is the pension lower than the salary?

The pension depends on the average wage of the last three years before retirement, not on yours, and on experience: 1% a year. With a salary of 30,000 UAH and 35 years of experience the pension is 19% of the salary, so the rest has to be covered by your own savings.

What are Kz and Ke?

Kz is the individual salary coefficient: the ratio of your salary to the national average. Ke is the experience coefficient: years of experience multiplied by 1%. The pension is the average wage for pensions × Kz × Ke.

How much should I save myself?

The “Your own savings” mode shows what you accumulate and how much that is per month. For example 3,000 UAH a month at 12% over 30 years gives 10,589,741 UAH (1,391,144 UAH in today’s money at 7% inflation). Enter a desired income — the calculator shows the required contribution.

How much should I save to get 30,000 UAH a month?

Without the state pension, at a 12% return and 7% inflation over 30 years you need to save about 9,652 UAH a month (payouts over 20 years, in today’s money). With the state pension the required contribution is lower: add it with the “Add the state pension” checkbox.

Does the calculator account for inflation, taxes and pension indexation?

Inflation yes: the amount and income are shown in today’s money. Taxes, fees and future changes of pension rules — no. The state pension is counted in today’s prices because it is indexed together with wages.

How accurate is the calculator?

It is an approximate estimate. The real Kz is calculated over the whole career, and the pension rules and amounts change. For an exact amount use the Pension Fund calculator, and treat savings as a forecast under your assumptions.
